# Credits (local fork)

Third-party assets added in this fork, with the attribution their licences
require. They are also listed in the main menu under **Mods → Credits**.

## Characters

- **Protogen** (`export/web/mods/characters/models/female_protogen/`)
  This work is based on "Female_Protogen"
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/female-protogen-839042536637493b9b7bd983d2a36e5c)
  by Jakermates (https://sketchfab.com/jaydidleedoo) licensed under CC-BY-4.0
  (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changes: animated procedurally in the browser (no changes to the model file).

## Classes

- **Pilot class mech** (`export/web/mods/classes/models/mech.glb`, built locally by `.tools/build_mech.sh`, not committed): based on "Animated Mech - Marceline Sentinel Armor (Marcy)" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-mech-marceline-sentinel-armor-marcy-9f169c3bcc61445993bf0ce269edc863) by RainOnTheMoon (https://sketchfab.com/RainOnTheMoon), licensed under CC-BY-4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: forearms cut off at the elbows (the game mounts its own weapons there), the chest opened into a cockpit, animations removed and replaced by code, textures downsized.

## Sounds

- **Zombie voices** (`export/web/mods/zombie-sounds/`): cut from "call of duty
  (yaa of the zombie) sound effect", uploaded to YouTube by kiouane
  (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5pQdqhYxd5U). The sounds are Call of Duty
  zombie audio, property of Activision / Treyarch.
- **Slide** (`export/web/mods/movement/slide.ogg`): trimmed from "slide on dirt
  sound effect", uploaded to YouTube by Z anims
  (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fJpFNdAWUhs). License not stated by the
  uploader.
