## Past seasons never expire (2026-10-22, user: "make it so old battle passes may be revisited… similar to how marvel rivals does it, just for future proofing")
- **Seasons index** `mods/pass/seasons.json` lists every season file, oldest first (`season.json` is Season 1). `loadSeasons(base)` returns `{list, current}`: `list` is every season whose `start` date (local) has passed, `current` (the live season) the newest of them. A season whose start is still to come stays hidden, so the next one can be dropped in ahead of time and goes live on its own date. `loadSeason()` still returns the live one. A new season: write `season-s2.json` (same shape, new `id`, dates, items with `pass: "s2"`; `.tools/build-season.py` is Season 1's generator, copy it and change `SEASON` and the output path), add it to seasons.json. The Rind OWN rule matches `pass/seasons.json` and `pass/season-s<n>.json`.
- **Store v2** `kino.mods.pass` = `{v:2, auto, track, seasons:{<id>:{xp, bought, premium, unlocked, granted}}, boosts, gums, archive}`. A v1 save (one flat season) migrates in `loadPass`; v1 `archive` summaries stay as they were.
- **Shared tokens** (Marvel Rivals' Chrono Tokens): `tokens(S,p)` = every season's tiers + bought tokens − every unlock, clamped at 0. Pass XP (`addXp(S,p,n)`) always goes to the live season; tiers past its last reward keep giving tokens (a "BONUS PASS TOKEN" announcement in a match), spendable on past seasons. Tier skips are bought at the live season's price.
- **Premium is per season** (`rec(p,season).premium`): a past season's premium pass can still be bought, at its own price, and hands over that season's held rewards.
- **Active pass**: `p.track = {id, live}`. `trackOf(S,p)` is the chosen past season while `live` is still the live season, else the live season, so a new season going live resets it (as in Marvel Rivals). `autoSpend` spends on the active pass, then the live season.
- **Rules API** now takes `S`: `unlock(S,season,p,id)`, `autoSpend(S,p)`, `buyToken(S,p)`, `addXp(S,p,n)`, `reconcile(S,p)`, `tokens(S,p)`; per-season helpers keep `(season,p)`. `window.kino.pass` gained `seasons`.
- **Menu**: with more than one season, a season strip above the header (newest first: SEASON n · LIVE/PAST · ACTIVE, rewards unlocked, PREMIUM) picks the season to view; a past season's header shows rewards unlocked, "Ran <dates> · never expires" and the live season's XP to the next token, plus MAKE ACTIVE PASS. With one season the tab looks as before.
- Verified: `test/pass-seasons.test.mjs` (index and date gating, v1 migration, shared tokens spent on a past season, active pass then live season, reset on a new season, bonus tokens, per-season premium, token purchase); the reaper-pass reconcile test moved to the new API; the tab in headless Chromium with a stubbed Season 2 at 1600 px and 390 px, no console errors.
