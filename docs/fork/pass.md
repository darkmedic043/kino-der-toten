# The Melon Pass (roadmap Phase 7, 2026-10-14)

User: "every tier has its own reward… use Black Ops 6 and 7's tiering system, with pages with a major reward once you claim the full page, able to be progressed auto with a toggle, or be able to spend tokens freely as you unlock more pages". So it follows BO6/BO7 rather than the PDF's 50-tier two-row track.

- **Season data** `mods/pass/season.json`, generated by `.tools/build-season.py` (re-run to rebuild; it replaces the season's entries): Season 1 "Melon Harvest" (2026-10-14 → 2026-12-14), 10 pages × 9 tier rewards + 1 major (page) reward = 90 tiers, 100 rewards, all different (the script asserts it; credits rewards have different amounts, XP tokens differ by kind and number of matches). `tierXp` 1,500, `premiumPrice` 1,000 credits, `tokenPrice` 150 (tier skip), pass XP: kill 10, headshot +5, 20 × round survived, daily challenge 1,000, weekly 3,000.
- **Rewards** are new pass-exclusive items written into the existing catalogues with `pass: "s1"`: 9 season camos (camos.json `season.*`, art in camo.js `SHOP_DRAW`: Vine Patch, Cornfield, Pumpkin Rind, Graveyard Fog, Blood Moon, Harvest Leaves, metallic Harvest Gold, and animated Witchfire (flame glow) and Moonlit (star glow)), 4 outfits (characters.json `outfit_s1_*`: Scarecrow, Melon Knight, Harvest Lord…), calling cards, reticles, hitmarkers, kill colours, HUD and menu themes, frames, sound sets, box glows (shop catalog items, `price: 0`), plus credits, double-XP tokens (`token` kind passXp/weaponXp/researchXp for the next n matches) and early gum unlocks (`gum`: Idle Eyes, Crate Power, Wall Power, On the House, Free Fire, Reign Drops, and Perkaholic as a page reward). The Shop never sells pass items (`listed`/`rotation`/`forSale` skip `pass`); owned ones show there for equipping. The Gunsmith's GLOBAL group gained a SEASON tab.
- **Rules** (`mods/pass/pass.js`, shared): each tier earned is a token (`tokens` = tiers + bought − unlocked). `unlock` spends one on any reward of an open page (`pageOpen`: page 1, or the previous page done), in any order; the reward is granted at once (items through the Phase 6 registry `grantReward`, credits into the wallet, tokens into `boosts`, gums into `gums`) unless it's premium and the pass isn't, in which case the page still progresses and the reward is held. All 9 unlocked → the major reward is granted (to everyone) and the next page opens. `buyPremium` grants every held reward; `buyToken` is a tier skip. `autoSpend` (the AUTO-PROGRESS toggle, default on) spends tokens on the first locked reward of the first open page. `granted` makes every reward grant once. `ensureSeason` archives an old season (owned items, gums, boosts stay). No credit refund on finishing (BO6/BO7 don't; the PDF's Marvel Rivals idea is open). Claiming is the unlock itself (no separate claim step).
- **Store** `kino.mods.pass` = `{season, xp, bought, auto, premium, unlocked, granted, boosts, gums, archive}` (cloud.js TRACKED; the Rind OWN list includes season.json).
- **Game** (`mods/pass/mod.js`): pass XP from kills and rounds (none in a cheat game) ×2 while a pass XP token is on; tokens are used at match `start` (`window.kino.boost` {passXp, weaponXp, researchXp}: weapon-levels and research read it); a tier-up announces the tier and, with auto-progress, the rewards it took; challenges add pass XP (credits mod calls `kino.pass.addXp`); pause menu line `#pass-panel`.
- **Menu** PASS tab (sidebar, `mods/pass/pass-tab.js` into `#pass-root`; token count on the sidebar button): season header (tier and bar, tokens, AUTO-PROGRESS, PREMIUM and BUY A TOKEN with confirm clicks), page strip (done ✓ / locked 🔒, sideways scroll), the page's 9 rewards (FREE / PREMIUM, unlocked / held / 1 token) beside the big page-reward card, a drawer with a preview (camos on the shared turning gun, outfits as a portrait, the rest as their tile) and UNLOCK · 1 TOKEN. Reward tiles come from `mods/shop/thumbs.js` (split out of the Shop tab).
