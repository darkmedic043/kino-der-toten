# Gobblegums (`mods/gobblegums`, roadmap Phase 2, 2026-10-14)

User's rules: no gums at the start of a match; gums come from a terminal app that rolls them slot-machine style; gums have rarities; a pack needs at least 10; rarer gums need a ratio of lower ones, rising like the perk price ladder; strong gums (Perkaholic) take far longer to unlock. Consumable gums were left undecided by the user ("not quite sure"), so gums are **not** consumed: the pack is the same every match. All numbers are in `gums.json`.

- **Data** (`gums.json`): `packMin` 10, `handSize` 3, `rollPrices` [500, 1000, 2000] by rolls this round, `rollsPerRound` 3. Tiers low → high: Rare, Epic (base 2, step 1), Legendary (3, 2), Ultra (5, 3). 18 gums with `level` unlocks (8 Rare at 1–6, 4 Epic at 8–16, 3 Legendary at 25–32, Ultra Reign Drops 45, Shopping Free 50, Perkaholic 70) and `type` instant / timed / conditional.
- **The ladder** (`gums.js`, shared by the menu and the mod): the k-th gum of a tier needs `base + step·(k−1)` gums of lower tiers in the pack (`tierNeed`, `packTiers`, `packProblems`, `canAdd`, `canRemove`). The pack is open-ended, so the ladder limits how *dense* a pack can be in rare gums, which sets the roll odds (rolls are uniform over the pack minus your hand). A valid pack first becomes possible at level 8 (8 Rare + 2 Epic).
- **Store**: `kino.mods.gobblegums` `{pack: [ids] | null}` (cloud-synced via `TRACKED` in cloud.js). `null` = automatic: every unlocked gum the ladder allows, lowest tier first (`activePack`). The first change in the menu saves an explicit list; RESET TO AUTOMATIC clears it.
- **Menu**: Armory → GOBBLEGUMS (`renderGums` in home.js): tiers top-down with "next one needs N lower-tier gums (pack has M)", click to add or remove (refused with the reason if it breaks the ladder), a pack panel with the per-tier ladder and problems, and gum details on hover.
- **In game** (`mod.js`): `window.kino.gobblegums` {status, roll, chew, give, live, armed, hand}. The hand is cleared on `reset`; rolls reset on `roundEnd`. Keys **N** chew the selected gum, **J** next (settings.js ACTIONS `gumChew`/`gumNext`, rebindable). HUD `#gum-hud` bottom left: three rarity-coloured slots, timers, ARMED conditionals. Effects: power-up gums drop the power-up **in front of you** (2026-10-14, user request: `dropPoint` puts it 110 units along the flat look direction, outside the 64-unit pickup radius, pulled in 30 short of a wall and snapped to the floor by a downward raycast; Reign Drops fans its six out in an arc of 0.42 rad steps), so you choose when to grab it; points; Sword Flay (melee ×5 via `beforeEnemyDamage`); Temporal Gift (wraps `session.powerup`, +30 s on timed power-ups); Idle Eyes / In Plain Sight (wrap `enemies.lureTarget` to the zombie's own position: they stand still); Shopping Free (wraps `session.spend`); Soda Fountain (watches `session.perks` for a new perk, then adds a random one); Perkaholic (`session.perks.add` for every perk in desktop.js `PERKS`, registering missing ones in `api.perkInfo` like the terminal does); Crate Power / Wall Power (wrap `mysteryBox.take` / `shop.buyWall`, then the next `giveWeapon` of that gun sets `upgraded` with full upgraded ammo). A synthesized pop on chew, pitched by rarity.
- **Terminal** (`desktop.js`): GUMS icon → GOBBLE.EXE. A full hand can roll too (2026-10-14, user): when the reel stops the app lists SWAP OUT <gum> for each held gum plus KEEP MY HAND (`gmPending`; `roll()` returns `full` and `commit(slot)`; no slot drops the rolled gum, still charged). A single slot reel (70 px rows, the pay line is the middle row): `opts.gumRoll()` charges and picks the gum, the strip of 46 random pack gums is built with the prize at row 42 and eased there over 2.6 s with ticks; `commit()` puts it in the hand when the reel stops. Enter/Space rolls. The side panel shows the result, pack, rolls this round, the next price and the hand; the button explains why it can't roll (no pack, hand full, come back next round, not enough points).
- **Co-op**: hands and packs are per player. Idle Eyes / In Plain Sight change zombie targeting, which runs on the host, so on a client they do nothing yet.
- Verified headlessly (Kino, level 70): automatic pack of 18; rolls charged 500/1000/2000 and the 4th was refused (hand full); Gift Card +5000; Perkaholic gave all eight perks; Shopping Free, Idle Eyes, Sword Flay timers and Crate Power ARMED on the HUD; the terminal reel landed the rolled gum on the pay line. Menu at level 30: removing Rares stops once the Epics would lack support.
- **More gums (2026-10-14, 25 in all)**: Equip Mint (Rare 7: grenades 4, claymores 2, monkeys 3), Pop Shocks (Rare 10, conditional with `charges` 5: a melee hit kills zombies within 160 of the one hit, cause `shock`), Power Vacuum (Epic 14, 4:00: `session.drops.tryDrop` is wrapped so a failed roll gets a 7% second chance with `pending` forced; the 4-a-round cap stays), Burned Out (Epic 18, 2 charges: when you take damage, zombies within 180 die, cause `fire`), Ephemeral Enhancement (Epic 20, 60 s: Pack-a-Punches the gun in hand and reverts it, ammo clamped to the base gun; refused before the gum is used if the gun can't be upgraded), On the House (Legendary 36: a random perk you lack), Free Fire (Legendary 40, 30 s: the mag in hand is kept full). Conditional gums may carry `charges` (`st.charges`; HUD shows `ARMED ×n`). Blasts run on `setTimeout(0)` because they fire from inside `enemies.hurt` / `session.damage`.
- **Not done**: Whimsical gums, gum art (rarity-coloured balls for now), controller and touch buttons for chew/next, the pause-menu pack switch from the roadmap, co-op sync of host-side effects.

- **Melon Pass gums (Season 1, 2026-10-14)**: pass-exclusive, unlocked only by owning them from the pass: Bountiful Harvest (rare, +50 points a kill for 60 s), Root Rot (rare, bullets slow 40% for 2 s for 30 s), Melon Smash (rare, 3 charges: melee kills burst within 150 for 60%), Vine Snare (epic, roots zombies within 260 for 4 s), Scarecrow (epic, 15 s: zombies within 600 lure away from you), Pumpkin Bomb (epic, a 2× zombie-health blast where you aim after 1 s), Second Helping (legendary, the next power-up activates twice; a timed one lasts 60 s), Harvest Moon (legendary, 45 s: every 8th kill drops a power-up), Reaper's Toll (ultra, 30 s: kills heal 10 and add 2 bullets), Cornucopia (ultra, a random box gun Pack-a-Punched). Verified each in a match (Pumpkin Bomb at the real aim point 600 away).

- **Pass-locked perks:** Perkaholic, On the House and Soda Fountain draw from `PERKS` filtered by `perkUnlocked` (terminal-booth/desktop.js), so Widow's Wine only comes from a gum once its Melon Pass item is owned.
- **Perkaholic gives 6 (2026-10-18, user: "if im going to keep adding more perks, it will be abit hard to balance")**: 6 random perks you don't have (`PERKAHOLIC` in mod.js; fewer if fewer are missing), was every perk. Free, so they don't raise the perk price. Verified on Kino: 6 perks from an empty set, three times.
- **Free Perk power-up, Soda Fountain twice (2026-11-06, user: "a free perk power up, cant drop from normal [kills], only certain things can spawn it, for now just the on the house gobblegum and reign drops. also ... soda fountain can be activated twice")**: `free_perk` is defined by the gobblegums mod's `prepare(data)` in `data.powerups` with `mod:'gobblegums'` and `special:true`. The kill drop deck (rules.js) has its own fixed list, so it never drops from zombies; mods that pick a random power-up from `data.powerups` (Der Eisendrache `side.js`, `side2.js`) skip `special` ones. On the House now drops one ahead of you (its `powerup` field) instead of handing out the perk, and Reign Drops' arc has it seventh. The engine's `collect()` passes a power-up type it doesn't know, whose def has `mod`, to mods as `powerup` {type, def} (with the grab sound and `map.onPowerup`). Every player's game runs that on a grab (co-op guests get it through the `pu` message), so everyone gets a random perk they don't have (`perkList`, pass-locked ones only once owned). The model is the perk-drink bottle `zombie_perk_bottle_jugg.glb`: a skinned viewmodel draws nothing as a plain clone, so `bakeBottle()` bakes it into a static mesh (`applyBoneTransform`), centres it, stands it upright, scales it to 21 tall and adds a gold emissive. Cheat `powerup free_perk` grabs one. Soda Fountain has `charges: 2`: each of the next two new perks brings an extra random one and spends a charge (`spendCharge`). Perks a gum or Free Perk hands out (`givePerk` adds them to `perksBefore`) don't set it off. Test: `scratch/melon/freeperk/test.mjs`.

## Season 1 pass gums (2026-11-06, user's own designs, `pass: "s2"`, `soon: true`)

Six gums for the next Melon Pass season (id `s2`). They are `soon` until it launches: never unlocked (`unlockedGums`, gums.js; the menu shows SOON), except with `localStorage['kino.dev.soon']='1'`. When the season ships, drop `soon` and the pass grants them like Season 0's gums. Code is the "Season 1 pass gums" block in `mod.js`; tunables live in `gums.json`.

| Gum | Rarity / type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Wonderbar! | ultra, conditional | Wraps `mysteryBox.start`: the next roll is a Wonder Weapon (`rarityOf` = `wonder`, lazily read from progression.json), taken from the roll's own pool (the player's box list) or any wonder weapon with a model; no teddy; then disarms |
| Time Out | ultra, timed 180 s | Kills every non-thrall zombie (cause `gum`, no score), sets `spawned=total` so the round ends, then holds the between-round countdown at the remaining time (`holdUntil`). Run by the host: a guest sends `{op:'timeout',d}` |
| Phoenix Up | legendary, instant, `coop` | `kino.coopReviveAll({phoenix:1})` (multiplayer mod) revives every downed teammate; their client saves the perks it lost at `goingDown` and gives them back on a `revived` message with `phoenix` |
| Profit Sharing | epic, timed 120 s, `coop` | `share` (0.25) of every gain from `session.addPoints` also goes to teammates within `radius` (750), both ways: chewing broadcasts `{op:'profit',d}`, and each client shares its own earnings while its own gum or a nearby chewer's is live. Sent as `{op:'pts',n}` every 0.5 s; received points skip addPoints (no double points, no loop) |
| Tactical Diffusion | rare, conditional | The next nuke the player picks up doesn't go off (`powerups.onCollect` returns `'defused'`) and everyone gets `bonus` (2000). The host decides: a guest's chew registers it (`{op:'defuse'}`), `kino.puBy` says who grabbed it, and the multiplayer `pu` broadcast carries `skip:1` so clients don't run the nuke |
| Anywhere But Here | rare, instant | Teleports to a random barrier's inside point (over 500 away if possible), then tosses zombies within `radius` (260) outward through `kino.thralls.order('toss')`, capped at walls, with the fx knockback and an explosion sound |

`coop: true` gums never roll (or come from gift gums) without a teammate, and every gum is refused while it would do nothing (`refuse(id)`: no teammate, nobody down, nowhere to go): the toast says why and the gum stays in hand. Co-op messages are type `gum` with `op` timeout/profit/pts/defuse/disarm (coop.md). Tested solo on Kino 2026-11-06 (`scratch/gums/solo.mjs`); Phoenix Up and Profit Sharing still need a real two-player test.
