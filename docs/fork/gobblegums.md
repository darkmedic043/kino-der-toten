# Gobblegums (`mods/gobblegums`, roadmap Phase 2, 2026-10-14)

User's rules: no gums at the start of a match; gums come from a terminal app that rolls them slot-machine style; gums have rarities; a pack needs at least 10; rarer gums need a ratio of lower ones, rising like the perk price ladder; strong gums (Perkaholic) take far longer to unlock. Consumable gums were left undecided by the user ("not quite sure"), so gums are **not** consumed: the pack is the same every match. All numbers are in `gums.json`.

- **Data** (`gums.json`): `packMin` 10, `handSize` 3, `rollPrices` [500, 1000, 2000] by rolls this round, `rollsPerRound` 3. Tiers low → high: Rare, Epic (base 2, step 1), Legendary (3, 2), Ultra (5, 3). 18 gums with `level` unlocks (8 Rare at 1–6, 4 Epic at 8–16, 3 Legendary at 25–32, Ultra Reign Drops 45, Shopping Free 50, Perkaholic 70) and `type` instant / timed / conditional.
- **The ladder** (`gums.js`, shared by the menu and the mod): the k-th gum of a tier needs `base + step·(k−1)` gums of lower tiers in the pack (`tierNeed`, `packTiers`, `packProblems`, `canAdd`, `canRemove`). The pack is open-ended, so the ladder limits how *dense* a pack can be in rare gums, which sets the roll odds (rolls are uniform over the pack minus your hand). A valid pack first becomes possible at level 8 (8 Rare + 2 Epic).
- **Store**: `kino.mods.gobblegums` `{pack: [ids] | null}` (cloud-synced via `TRACKED` in cloud.js). `null` = automatic: every unlocked gum the ladder allows, lowest tier first (`activePack`). The first change in the menu saves an explicit list; RESET TO AUTOMATIC clears it.
- **Menu**: Armory → GOBBLEGUMS (`renderGums` in home.js): tiers top-down with "next one needs N lower-tier gums (pack has M)", click to add or remove (refused with the reason if it breaks the ladder), a pack panel with the per-tier ladder and problems, and gum details on hover.
- **In game** (`mod.js`): `window.kino.gobblegums` {status, roll, chew, give, live, armed, hand}. The hand is cleared on `reset`; rolls reset on `roundEnd`. Keys **N** chew the selected gum, **J** next (settings.js ACTIONS `gumChew`/`gumNext`, rebindable). HUD `#gum-hud` bottom left: three rarity-coloured slots, timers, ARMED conditionals. Effects: power-up drops at your feet (`powerups.spawn`); points; Sword Flay (melee ×5 via `beforeEnemyDamage`); Temporal Gift (wraps `session.powerup`, +30 s on timed power-ups); Idle Eyes / In Plain Sight (wrap `enemies.lureTarget` to the zombie's own position: they stand still); Shopping Free (wraps `session.spend`); Soda Fountain (watches `session.perks` for a new perk, then adds a random one); Perkaholic (`session.perks.add` for every perk in desktop.js `PERKS`, registering missing ones in `api.perkInfo` like the terminal does); Crate Power / Wall Power (wrap `mysteryBox.take` / `shop.buyWall`, then the next `giveWeapon` of that gun sets `upgraded` with full upgraded ammo). A synthesized pop on chew, pitched by rarity.
- **Terminal** (`desktop.js`): GUMS icon → GOBBLE.EXE. A single slot reel (70 px rows, the pay line is the middle row): `opts.gumRoll()` charges and picks the gum, the strip of 46 random pack gums is built with the prize at row 42 and eased there over 2.6 s with ticks; `commit()` puts it in the hand when the reel stops. Enter/Space rolls. The side panel shows the result, pack, rolls this round, the next price and the hand; the button explains why it can't roll (no pack, hand full, come back next round, not enough points).
- **Co-op**: hands and packs are per player. Idle Eyes / In Plain Sight change zombie targeting, which runs on the host, so on a client they do nothing yet.
- Verified headlessly (Kino, level 70): automatic pack of 18; rolls charged 500/1000/2000 and the 4th was refused (hand full); Gift Card +5000; Perkaholic gave all eight perks; Shopping Free, Idle Eyes, Sword Flay timers and Crate Power ARMED on the HUD; the terminal reel landed the rolled gum on the pay line. Menu at level 30: removing Rares stops once the Epics would lack support.
- **Not done**: Whimsical gums, gum art (rarity-coloured balls for now), controller and touch buttons for chew/next, the pause-menu pack switch from the roadmap, co-op sync of host-side effects.
