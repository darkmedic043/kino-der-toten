# Research: perk and ammo mod augments (`mods/research`, roadmap Phase 5, 2026-10-14)

Black Ops 6 Zombies style. The roadmap PDF assumed no ammo mods existed and four perks; by now there were eight of each (ammo-mods mod, perks-plus), so all 16 items got augments: 2 Major + 2 Minor each, 64 in all (`research.json`; numbers are first drafts).

- **Rules** (`research.js`, shared by menu and game): one research at a time (switching keeps progress); research XP = kill 10, headshot +5, round survived 25 × round, ×1.5 while its item is active (the perk is owned, or the ammo mod is on the gun in hand); Minor 1,000, Major 2,000; opens at player level 11 (BO6's; via `unlockLvl`, so a player prestige doesn't close it). Equip one Major and one Minor per item.
- **Store** `kino.mods.research` = `{active, progress:{augId:xp}, done:[augId], equip:{itemId:{major,minor}}}`, its own key (cloud.js TRACKED; the Rind OWN list includes research.json). In a match it's kept in memory and saved 1.5 s after the last gain, on completion and on unload; re-read on `start`.
- **Armory → Research** (`pane.js`, home.js `drawResearch`, between Gobblegums and Classes): items by group, the active research with its bar, the item's Major/Minor cards (RESEARCH / RESUME / EQUIP / UNEQUIP). Touch sized; one column under 760 px. Adding a sixth Armory tab overflowed narrow screens: the tab bar now scrolls sideways and `body>main{min-width:0}` stops the grid stretching the page (home.css). Landscape 844×390: no overflow on any pane. Portrait 390 was already 587 px wide before this (Gobblegums too); not fixed.
- **Game** (`mod.js`): equipped augments are locked in at match start (`lockIn` on load and `start`); perk augments count only while you own that perk (`stat(key)`), ammo mod augments only for that mod. Completion: announce + toast. Pause menu: `#research-panel` under the Gunsmith panel (active research, locked-in augments).
- **Perk augments**: Quick Revive (Dying Wish: once a round a downing hit leaves 1 health and 3 s untouchable; Durable Heal: regen ×2; Quick Recovery: regen from 1 s; Karmic Return: kills heal 3), Juggernog (Thick Skin: first hit each round halved; Probiotic +50 / Reinforced +15 max health by re-wrapping player-health's `maxHealth` getter; Hard Target −5% damage), Speed Cola (Supercharged: class action skill charges 25% faster through `window.kino.chargeMul`, read by the classes mod; Phantom Reload: stowed guns refill their mag after 3 s; Quick Mag: a kill mid-reload cuts 25%; Fast Hands: reload −10%), Double Tap (Hollow Points headshots +25%; Double Impact 10% of bullets hit twice; Hardened Rounds +5%; Bounty +5 points per headshot hit), Stamin-Up (Hot Foot: sprinting burns zombies within 75; Free Faller: no fall damage and +20% speed for 3 s after a fall; Runner / Light Step +5% walk / sprint, wrapping perks-plus's speed getters), PhD Flopper (Flash Point: the classes mod emits `abilityUsed`; PhD Slider: sliding into a zombie; Bigger Bang: `window.kino.phdScale`; Delayed Reaction: an echo on `phdBlast`; perks-plus exposes `kino.phd.flop(at,{power,echo})`), Deadshot (Dead Head burst on headshot kills; Critical Mass every 5th headshot ×3; Sharpshooter +10%; Marked +10% for 3 s), Mule Kick (Ammo Mule +5% reserve every 10 s; Third Wheel +15% with the third-slot gun; Light Load +5% speed with three guns; Scavenger 5% chance to refill a tenth of the reserve).
- **Ammo mod augments** are data: `set`/`add`/`mul`/`max` on the mod's TUNE numbers through `ammoModTune`, `chance` × through `ammoModProc`, `spread` {n, r} (Ice Cloud, Wildfire: the proc is re-triggered on neighbours with `kino.ammoMods.trigger`) and `refill` (Ammo Theorem) on the new `ammoModFired` event (ammo-mods emits it after a landed proc).
- Verified in a match (GPU, headless): 60 kills = 600 XP; with Juggernog owned 27 more finished a 1,000 XP Minor; Probiotic made Juggernog 300 max health; Thick Skin took a 40 hit as 20 then 40; Seismic made Shatter's radius 154 (from 110); Frostbite raised Cryo's proc chance 0.07 → 0.091; Ice Cloud chilled the 3 zombies within 140 and not the far one; progress survived a reload and a progression save; the phone pane equipped by tap.
- **Balance** (PDF gate "no augment makes an early round trivial"): research opens at level 11, a Major takes ~200 kills, and perk augments need the perk, ammo mod augments need a mod bought with the power on, so none touch the Phase 1 pistol starts. Strongest on paper: Dying Wish (a free save each round), Double Impact, Supercharged with the class meter. Not simulated yet; tune in research.json after play.
- Not done: Moon's own perk path (`moon-session.js`) gives the same perk ids, so perk augments should work there, but it wasn't tested; co-op: effects on zombies (Ice Cloud, Hot Foot, Dead Head) only bite on the host's zombies, like the ammo mods themselves.
