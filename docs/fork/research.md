# Research: perk and ammo mod augments (`mods/research`, roadmap Phase 5, 2026-10-14)

**BO7 rework (same day, user: "2 major and minor is a bit small… a linear tree with a thing at the end that gives you the ability to equip 2 minor augments… 4 for major, 4 for minor… take the augments from BO7"):** every item now has a **linear track of 8 augments** (4 Major, 4 Minor, alternating Minor/Major in BO7's order, 128 in all). Names come from BO7 (the official augment breakdown, via the Gameshedge guide's full list); Light Mend uses BO6's names; Mule Kick and Shatter Blast aren't in BO7, so theirs are the fork's. BO7 effects that need things the game lacks are adapted (no armour plates: the plate augments are damage reduction and max health; no tactical sprint or equipment-while-sprinting; BO7's elites → hellhounds and specials for Big Game, which here means "always procs on them"). The research.json `desc` is what each one does here.
- Rules (`research.js`): only the next augment on a track can be researched (`nextOnTrack`, `startResearch` refuses others); finishing one starts the next on the same track automatically; costs rise along the track (`trackCost` 2,000…12,000, 50,000 per item since 2026-10-15; was 500…3,000). Finishing all 8 = **mastery** (`mastered`): a third equip slot, `minor2`. `toggleEquip` fills Minor, then Minor 2 once mastered, and clears slots that point at augments that no longer exist (saves from the 2+2 version: their done ids that still exist keep counting, the rest are ignored).
- Pane: the 8 steps as a vertical track (numbered, Major steps edged in the item colour, the active one ringed), slots MAJOR / MINOR / MINOR 2 · MASTERY, a mastery banner; items show n/8 or MASTERED.
- New effect hooks: Double Time (fire wait shortened per shot via `session.shots`), Quick Swap (wraps `switchWeapon`), Prestidigitation (reserve refunded when the reload lands), Brain Rot `bite`/`fumes` TUNE params (ammo-mods), Thermal Shock / Petroleum / Firebomb / Contact Burn / Concussion in the research mod's `ammoModFired` handler, speed boosts as timed `boost()` entries added to the speed getters.
- Verified: Node (order enforced, auto-advance through 7 steps to MASTERY, Minor 2 equips only once mastered, an old 2+2 save loads); in a match with a mastered Juggernog (Iron Core + Retaliation + Hardened Plates): a 50 hit took 38 under half health, body shots ×1.15 under 35%; Dead First ×2 headshots only at full health; Big Game: Cryo always procs on a hellhound; Super Serum/Caustic Fumes changed Brain Rot's bite .25→.375 and fumes 0→.1; Thermal Shock dealt exactly 20% when the chill ended; a Petroleum pool burned a neighbour; Double Time finished and Double or Nothing started on its own.

## First version (2+2 per item), kept for the record

Black Ops 6 Zombies style. The roadmap PDF assumed no ammo mods existed and four perks; by now there were eight of each (ammo-mods mod, perks-plus), so all 16 items got augments: 2 Major + 2 Minor each, 64 in all (`research.json`; numbers are first drafts).

- **Rules** (`research.js`, shared by menu and game): one research at a time (switching keeps progress); research XP = kill 10, headshot +5, round survived 25 × round, ×1.5 while its item is active (the perk is owned, or the ammo mod is on the gun in hand); Minor 1,000, Major 2,000; opens at player level 11 (BO6's; via `unlockLvl`, so a player prestige doesn't close it). Equip one Major and one Minor per item.
- **Store** `kino.mods.research` = `{active, progress:{augId:xp}, done:[augId], equip:{itemId:{major,minor}}}`, its own key (cloud.js TRACKED; the Rind OWN list includes research.json). In a match it's kept in memory and saved 1.5 s after the last gain, on completion and on unload; re-read on `start`.
- **Armory → Research** (`pane.js`, home.js `drawResearch`, between Gobblegums and Classes): items by group, the active research with its bar, the item's Major/Minor cards (RESEARCH / RESUME / EQUIP / UNEQUIP). Touch sized; one column under 760 px. Adding a sixth Armory tab overflowed narrow screens: the tab bar now scrolls sideways and `body>main{min-width:0}` stops the grid stretching the page (home.css). Landscape 844×390: no overflow on any pane. Portrait 390 was already 587 px wide before this (Gobblegums too); not fixed.
- **Game** (`mod.js`): equipped augments are locked in at match start (`lockIn` on load and `start`); perk augments count only while you own that perk (`stat(key)`), ammo mod augments only for that mod. Completion: announce + toast. Pause menu: `#research-panel` under the Gunsmith panel (active research, locked-in augments).
- **Perk augments**: Quick Revive (Dying Wish: once a round a downing hit leaves 1 health and 3 s untouchable; Durable Heal: regen ×2; Quick Recovery: regen from 1 s; Karmic Return: kills heal 3), Juggernog (Thick Skin: first hit each round halved; Probiotic +50 / Reinforced +15 max health by re-wrapping player-health's `maxHealth` getter; Hard Target −5% damage), Speed Cola (Supercharged: class action skill charges 25% faster through `window.kino.chargeMul`, read by the classes mod; Phantom Reload: stowed guns refill their mag after 3 s; Quick Mag: a kill mid-reload cuts 25%; Fast Hands: reload −10%), Double Tap (Hollow Points headshots +25%; Double Impact 10% of bullets hit twice; Hardened Rounds +5%; Bounty +5 points per headshot hit), Stamin-Up (Hot Foot: sprinting burns zombies within 75; Free Faller: no fall damage and +20% speed for 3 s after a fall; Runner / Light Step +5% walk / sprint, wrapping perks-plus's speed getters), PhD Flopper (Flash Point: the classes mod emits `abilityUsed`; PhD Slider: sliding into a zombie; Bigger Bang: `window.kino.phdScale`; Delayed Reaction: an echo on `phdBlast`; perks-plus exposes `kino.phd.flop(at,{power,echo})`), Deadshot (Dead Head burst on headshot kills; Critical Mass every 5th headshot ×3; Sharpshooter +10%; Marked +10% for 3 s), Mule Kick (Ammo Mule +5% reserve every 10 s; Third Wheel +15% with the third-slot gun; Light Load +5% speed with three guns; Scavenger 5% chance to refill a tenth of the reserve).
- **Ammo mod augments** are data: `set`/`add`/`mul`/`max` on the mod's TUNE numbers through `ammoModTune`, `chance` × through `ammoModProc`, `spread` {n, r} (Ice Cloud, Wildfire: the proc is re-triggered on neighbours with `kino.ammoMods.trigger`) and `refill` (Ammo Theorem) on the new `ammoModFired` event (ammo-mods emits it after a landed proc).
- Verified in a match (GPU, headless): 60 kills = 600 XP; with Juggernog owned 27 more finished a 1,000 XP Minor; Probiotic made Juggernog 300 max health; Thick Skin took a 40 hit as 20 then 40; Seismic made Shatter's radius 154 (from 110); Frostbite raised Cryo's proc chance 0.07 → 0.091; Ice Cloud chilled the 3 zombies within 140 and not the far one; progress survived a reload and a progression save; the phone pane equipped by tap.
- **Balance** (PDF gate "no augment makes an early round trivial"): research opens at level 11, a Major takes ~200 kills, and perk augments need the perk, ammo mod augments need a mod bought with the power on, so none touch the Phase 1 pistol starts. Strongest on paper: Dying Wish (a free save each round), Double Impact, Supercharged with the class meter. Not simulated yet; tune in research.json after play.
- Not done: Moon's own perk path (`moon-session.js`) gives the same perk ids, so perk augments should work there, but it wasn't tested; co-op: effects on zombies (Ice Cloud, Hot Foot, Dead Head) only bite on the host's zombies, like the ammo mods themselves.
- **Never stalls (2026-10-15, user: "when you unlock a tier … it should automatically go to the next … so you can stay in game and get more")**: finishing an augment already moved to the next on its track; now a mastered track moves on to the next unmastered item (same kind first: perk, then ammo mod), and XP past the cost carries into the next augment (it was dropped). The toast names what's being researched now. Node test: 9 completions in a row ran Quick Revive to mastery and on into Juggernog's Retaliation.
- **Slower research (2026-10-15, user: "should take way longer to max out a research")**: every `trackCost` step ×4 (2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 / 5,000 / 6,000 / 8,000 / 10,000 / 12,000 = 50,000 per item, was 12,500). A round-20 match with ~300 kills earns about 8,000 research XP (×1.5 while the item is active), so one item takes roughly 4–6 such matches and all 16 about 80–100.
- **BO7 perk tracks (2026-10-15)**: Vulture Aid, Widow's Wine, Wisp Tea, Death Perception, Elemental Pop and Melee Macchiato got tracks of their eight BO7 augments (22 items, 176 augments), Minor/Major alternating as the others; effect keys `va.*`, `ww.*`, `wt.*`, `dp.*`, `ep.*`, `mm.*`, read by `mods/perks-plus/bo7-perks.js` (gameplay.md). Adaptations: Fetid Upgr-aid charges the class action skill, Armor-matic heals 15 per drop, Bird's Eye View draws off-screen arrows, Treasure Hunter drops power-ups from special enemies, Mocha Maul (BO7: your melee weapon replaces the punch) is a shockwave on melee kills, Imperil Peach / Rainbow Pop / Death Stare work on any ammo-mod proc.
- **Prismatic Punch and Dam-A-Nade tracks (2026-10-18)**: the first two Reaper Collection perks switched on have the fork's own tracks (24 items), effect keys `pp.*` and `dn.*`, read by `mods/perks-plus/reaper-perks.js`; their mod's double-buy upgrades are Majors (Afterglow, Demolition Expert). The augments and numbers: reaper-perks.md, "Implemented".
- **Magnet Margarita track (2026-10-19)**: effect keys `mm.*` (pull, slots, reach, pulse, linger, bonus, restock, twin), read by `mods/perks-plus/magnet.js`; Deep Pockets (+2 stash slots) is the user's "hold more power ups" augment, Attraction the mod's double-buy bonus drops. Numbers: reaper-perks.md, "Magnet Margarita (implemented)".
- **Slower again (2026-10-16, user: "a bit harder… even more XP, should take a few games to max out")**: `trackCost` ×3: 6,000 / 9,000 / 12,000 / 15,000 / 18,000 / 24,000 / 30,000 / 36,000 = 150,000 per item. Round XP grows with the round (25 × round), so long games had been mastering an item in one or two: a round-30 match (~800 kills) earns about 21,000 (×1.5 while the item is active, ~32,000), so an item now takes about five of those, three or four for round-40+ games.
