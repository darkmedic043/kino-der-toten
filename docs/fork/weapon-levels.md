# Weapon levels, attachments and scopes

## Scopes (`mods/scopes`, all maps)

The exported viewmodels kept scope lenses opaque, so aiming a scoped gun showed a black disc.
- **Glass**: materials matching `lens(?!_interior)|reflex_lens` become transparent (0.1). The model's dot cards (`red_dot|reflex_red|aimpoint_red|scope_pka_crosshair`) have mixed backgrounds (some glowed as red blobs), so they're hidden. A CSS `#reflex-dot` is drawn at the screen centre while aiming a reflex/red dot sight (aim > .85).
- **Sight alignment** (reflex/red dot/holo, incl. mounted BO3 sights): `measure()` finds the dot/lens centroid in view space and the pivot is shifted so it sits on the screen centre. It re-measures every frame while aim > .6 and eases toward the result (a one-off measure at aim .97 caught the BO3 ADS pose and mounted parts still settling). `measure()` first calls `view.pivot.updateMatrixWorld(true)`: `view.update` moves the pivot but only refreshes the gun below it, so without that the measure saw last frame's offset and the loop settled halfway. `kino.scopes` exposes `align`, `measure`, `view` for tests.
- **Magnified optics** (`BUILT_IN`: Dragunov `pso`, L96A1 `duplex`, G11 `lps`, AUG `acog`; plus `def.scope` from attachments: `acog`, `ir`): once `view.aim` > .92, `host.hideViewmodel` is set and a canvas overlay draws a black housing with a round window and that optic's reticle (dark lines get a faint light halo so they read in dark rooms). Zoom per kind in `MAGNIFIED` (it also sets the built-in guns' `adsFov`). Sway moves the real aim (camera rotation, undone each frame). Holding Shift holds your breath for 3 s (sway ×0.08), then a 1.6 s gasp (×2.2). IR adds a CSS grayscale/contrast filter on the canvas and white emissive on zombie materials (restored after).
- **Sight alignment** (unmagnified): at full aim, the centroid of the visible sight's dot/lens vertices is measured in view space and the gun pivot is shifted so it sits on the screen centre, and at least 9 units from the eye. Gotchas: every surface shares one vertex buffer, so iterate the mesh's **index**. Hidden attachments are collapsed onto bones scaled 1e-6, so skip meshes whose dominant-weight bone chain has a ~0 scale.

## Weapon levels (`mods/weapon-levels`)

- `attachments.json`: XP (kill 10, headshot +5), curve (120·L^1.25), slots (optic, muzzle, mag, under), `mountTags` (rails shown with any optic), `builtInOptic` (no optic slot), and the attachment catalog in **unlock order**. Each entry has the tags it shows (plus `extraTags`), stats (`damageMul`, `rangeMul`, `clipMul` (maxAmmo grows by the same amount), `reloadMul`, `adsFov`/`adsFovMul`, `headMul`, `hipSpreadMul`, `suppressed`), `scope` and `underbarrel`.
- An attachment's `tags` are alternatives: only the first one the gun has is shown (the Galil carries both the Elbit and PKA reflex sights). `extraTags` and rail `mountTags` are shown when present.
- A weapon's attachments are the catalog entries whose tags appear in its default `hideTags` (BO1 viewmodels carry every attachment, hidden by tag). Level n unlocks the (n−1)th; max level = count + 1.
- `gunsmith.js` (shared by the mod and the main menu): profile fields `weaponXp[id]={level,xp}` and `attachments[id]=[ids]` (one per slot) in `kino.mods.progression`, so they're cloud-synced. `applyAttachments` restores each def (and its `upgrade`) from a pristine snapshot, then applies stats, removes shown tags from `hideTags`, sets `scope`, `attachments` and `hipSpread`, and builds `def.attachment` for underbarrels. The upstream key-5 toggle (`session.toggleAttachment`) then works without Pack-a-Punch. Templates: the M16's upgraded GL and the AUG's upgraded Masterkey (own animations only for those two guns; others keep their normal ones), with base stats GL 450 dmg 1+6 and Masterkey 150×8, 4+20.
- The mod: XP on `kill` when the last damage cause was `bullet`/`projectile`/`thunder`, or `explosion` while holding a launcher (not melee, not remote, not cheats). New attachments are auto-equipped if their slot is free, with a toast. It wraps `view.equip` to re-show bones of attachment tags that the current def doesn't hide (viewmodels are cached, so earlier hidden scales stick). Suppressed shots play at 0.28 volume (it wraps `audio.weapon`/`play`). Shows a `#weapon-level` HUD, and a pause-menu `#gunsmith-panel` for the gun in hand (refreshed when `#menu` unhides). `window.kino.weaponLevels.grant(id, xp)` is for testing.
- Main menu: `renderDetail` in home.js appends `renderGunsmith` for the focused weapon.
- Verified headlessly: an M16 with reflex+suppressor+extmag+GL has clip 45, damage 85, adsFov 46, and key 5 → "M16 · Grenade Launcher" 1/6. Three kills from 110 XP → level 2, Reflex unlocked and equipped. Sniper/ACOG/LPS overlays and the reflex/red dot alignment were checked in screenshots on the M16, Galil, AK-74u and MP5K. npm test passes (65).
- **Not done**: flamethrower underbarrel (no flame weapon exists), variable zoom, attachments on third-person/world models.

## Flamethrower underbarrel (`mods/weapon-levels/flamer.js`)

BO1 reference (Call of Duty wiki via search): an underbarrel on assault rifles except the G11, 100 fuel per tank at 4 per trigger pull (25 bursts), 600 RPM, ~12.1 m range, 50 damage per hit. Here: `flamerDef` builds the key-5 attachment def (clip 25 + 75 spare, fireTime .1, damage 1 so the hitscan does nothing). On each `session.shots` increment it `scorch`es every zombie within 475 units in a 22° cone, with line of sight and a wall-limited reach: 45 damage (cause `flame`, which counts toward weapon XP) plus ignition, which burns for 3 s at 55/s with flames on the body. Visuals use the game's own flipbooks: `fxt_fire_flame_base_01` (4×4 burn-out, additive), `fxt_fire_flame_lick` (16 variations), and `fxt_smk_flamethrower` (8×8 fireball→smoke, alpha) at the stream's end. They're emitted at ~95/s while firing from the nozzle's screen position (`tag_ft_flash`, then `tag_flame_unit`, then `tag_flash`, projected with a FOV-51 view camera and unprojected into the world). Sound: a synthesized looped roar into `audio.master` (the game data has no flamethrower loop), and the normal shot cue is muted. There's a pilot-light sprite on `tag_flamer_pilot_light` when the model has one. Tags: `tag_flame_unit` plus the bracket, bottle, button, ring, heat guard and pilot light.

## Gunsmith view (`mods/weapon-levels/gunsmith-view.js`)

A full-screen, BO7-style Gunsmith opened from Main menu → Loadout (weapon detail → OPEN GUNSMITH, with ◀ ▶ through every gun that has attachments) and from the pause menu (the gun in hand). It has its own WebGLRenderer. `loadModel(def.model)` (the viewmodel gun, which carries every attachment) is turned so the barrel runs along +X and centred; parts are shown or hidden by bone scale from `previewDef`. Left: slot rows with SVG leader lines (dashed; solid orange when selected) to screen-projected markers on the part each slot edits. The marker is the centroid of the fitted part's skinned vertices (index-based, cached in gun space), with a bone fallback for empty slots. Selecting a slot eases the camera onto that part and pulses its meshes (emissive, found by each mesh's dominant bone; materials are shared with the game, so they're restored on close). Right: that slot's options with pros/cons; hovering previews the part on the model and shows green/red stat deltas. Esc/BACK closes; clicks don't reach the game. `available()` uses the pristine `hideTags`, because applying attachments un-hides tags. Before that fix, equipped attachments vanished from the Gunsmith and the weapon's max level shrank.

## Variable zoom, third person, co-op, Pack-a-Punch layer (2026-09-30, later)

- **Variable Zoom** (`vzoom`, optic, `builtIn: true`): the only optic kind allowed on `builtInOptic` guns (`available()` requires `builtInOptic.includes(id) === !!a.builtIn`). It shows `tag_scope_vzoom` and hides the stock scope via `replaces` (`tag_dragunov_scope`, the L96's `tag_scope`, `tag_scope_g11`), which pushes those tags onto `hideTags`. In the scopes mod, kind `vzoom` draws a mil-dot duplex with a magnification readout. While scoped, the mouse wheel (captured on window before the game's wheel-to-switch) steps `ZOOMS` 4×/6×/8×/10× (FOV 24/16/12/9) by writing `adsFov` on the def and its upgrade. Verified: 16 → 9 after two wheel-ups, and the weapon didn't switch.
- **Third person**: `holdWeapon` already hides parts from the current def's `hideTags`, which attachments rewrite. The characters mod's hold key now includes `data.weapons[id].attachments`, so changes re-hold the gun. Verified: an M16 with ACOG + suppressor + M203 in third person.
- **Co-op**: the pose `w` is now `id:u?:attachments` (older `id:u` still parses). Teammates' held guns get `window.kino.weaponLevels.hideTagsFor(id, ids)` (a `previewDef` from the pristine def), so each player shows their own attachments.
- **Pack-a-Punch shot layer** (`mods/pap-sound`): the upgraded-weapon "pew" isn't in this export's audio (nothing matches pap/upgrad in the manifest, and the game dumps aren't in this checkout). So `audio.weapon('shot')` on upgraded, non-wonder guns adds a synthesized layer: saw + detuned square sweeping 1.9 kHz → 260 Hz over 0.13 s through a band-pass sweeping 2.6 k → 700, quieter on rapid fire. The real layer is now in place: `pap-layer.ogg` and `pap-layer2.ogg`, two 0.55 s variants cut from a BO1 recording the user downloaded (youtube dSUVsrvncyU, via yt-dlp into ~/Downloads/pap_shot.wav; loudnorm −16 LUFS, 0.13 s fade). One is picked at random at ±3% pitch, gain 0.6 (0.45 on rapid fire). The synthesized sweep is only a fallback if they fail to load.

## Fixes after user testing (2026-09-30, later)

- **Weapon XP reverting**: the progression mod saved its own stale copy of the profile 1.5 s after each kill, overwriting weapon XP (it looked like "not levelling, bar resets"). Its `save` now takes `weaponXp`/`attachments` from storage first, and the main menu's `save` takes the stored `weaponXp`. Tests must wait out that 1.5 s debounce (verified: 12 kills + 3.5 s → level 2, 170 XP).
- **No auto-equip**: unlocking only toasts "… unlocked · equip it in the Gunsmith" (the user found auto-equip snappy).
- **Reflex glass**: dot cards are hidden **per mesh** on every viewmodel copy. A per-material "already patched" skip left them visible on later copies (a dark dotted disc blocking the sight).
- **Bigger mags and reloads**: reload animations move `tag_clip` (the stock mag); fitted mags hang off their own bones. While one is fitted, the stock mag meshes (dominant bone `tag_clip`) are hidden, and the fitted mag's bone is driven each frame from `tag_clip`'s animated world matrix × their rest offset (from `skeleton.boneInverses`). Only mags that an attachment added are moved: the stock-hidden sets (base and upgrade) are snapshotted before `apply()`. Built-in bigger mags keep their own animation (the Pack-a-Punched Commando's dual mag, the PM63 upgrade's extended mag); moving the Predator's had shifted it up. When a Pack-a-Punched gun shows its own bigger mag *and* a mag attachment is fitted (the user's Commando with extmag → Predator), the fitted mag follows the built-in mag's bone instead of `tag_clip`: the PaP reload animates that bone, and following `tag_clip` left the mag sticking out of the top. The built-in mag's meshes are hidden (its bone keeps scale 1, so its matrix stays usable). Verified with side renders mid-reload (M16, AK-74u, Galil dualmag, Commando extmag plain and Pack-a-Punched).
- **Gunsmith markers**: empty slots use a measured home (a representative attachment fitted for a moment at load). Only tags the gun hides by default count as slot parts (the M16's stock handguard is skinned to `tag_heat_guard`).
- **Loadout**: GUNSMITH buttons on the Primary/Secondary cards; the detail panel's Gunsmith is only for the weapon in the selected slot; hovering the grid previews another gun and leaving returns to the equipped one; the Gunsmith's arrows cycle only primary/secondary (hidden if there's one).
- **Iron sights with optics**: `attachments.json` `ironSights` maps a gun to its iron-sight bones (front and rear), which `foldIronSights` hides when any optic is fitted, so the front post doesn't show through the sight window. That covers the Galil, MP5K, M14, M16, Commando, FAMAS, Spectre, FAL, MPL, AK-74u, HK21 and RPK. On the M16/Commando the bone is the carry handle plus the front post, and on the FAMAS it's the handle; the optics don't hang off those bones (verified hip + ADS, 2026-09-30).
- **Box and wall guns** (2026-10-01): attachments are saved per gun and applied to `data.weapons` at load, so a gun from the box or a wall comes with them (verified: a Galil with reflex + dualmag rolled from the real box arrived with both, rear sight folded). The main-menu Gunsmith's arrows cycle every gun with attachments (loadout first), and the header labels each PRIMARY / SECONDARY / BOX / WALL GUN (`label` option of `openGunsmith`), so guns can be set up before they're found. The pause-menu Gunsmith still edits the gun in hand.

## Camos (`mods/weapon-levels/camo.js`, `camos.json`; 2026-10-01)

- Per-gun challenges counted on eligible gun kills (same rule as weapon XP, but for any gun): `kills`, `headshots`, `low` (prone or crouched), `moving` (sliding or diving), `multi` (another kill with the same gun within 1.5 s). Saved in `profile.camo[id] = {p:{counts}, on:camoId}`. Each camo has a challenge and count; `mastery` (Gold) unlocks when every other camo is done. Unlocks toast "… camo unlocked · equip it in the Gunsmith" (never auto-equipped).
- Placeholders are drawn on canvases (woodland, tiger, digital, urban, red tiger, gold). A real camo is `"texture": "camos/<file>.png"` (tileable, next to camos.json), replacing `procedural`; `scale` is UV repeats and `mix` is coverage; `metal` raises metalness and lowers roughness (Gold).
- Painting: `applyCamo(root, camo)` swaps body materials for cached clones (keyed on original uuid + camo id; the original is kept in `userData.camoOf`, so null restores). The shader adds the camo after `map_fragment`: `mix(base, camo × (.3 + luminance × 1.9), mix)` on `vMapUv × scale`, so the gun's shading and wear show through. Body = has a map and the name doesn't match the SKIP list (sights, lenses, suppressors, underbarrels, straps, mag tape, mounts, rails, iron sights, hands…).
- Where it shows: the viewmodel (`view.equip` wrapper), the box display (wrapped `mysteryBox.display`), the third-person held gun (the characters mod's hold key includes `def.camo`), co-op (pose `w` gains a 4th field, the camo id) and the Gunsmith preview. `data.weapons[id].camo` is set in `apply()`. `window.kino.camo.apply(root, id)`.
- Gunsmith: a CAMO row under the attachments (swatch, `n / 6 UNLOCKED`); its options list each camo with its challenge and a progress bar, locked until earned, and hovering previews it on the 3D gun. The stats panel is hidden on that row.
- Profile saves keep camo: weapon-levels `save`, progression `write` (takes stored `camo`), and the menu's `save` (stored progress, menu's `on`).
- Counts raised at the user's request (2026-10-01): Woodland 500 kills, Tiger 200 headshots, Digital 150 low, Urban 75 moving, Red Tiger 40 multi-kills; Gold still needs all five.
- **Diamond** (`classMastery`, 2026-10-01): unlocks for every gun in a class once every gun in that class has Gold (all challenge camos done). Classes are shared in camo.js (`weaponClass`, `CLASSES`, `classOf`, `classGuns`; home.js imports them): AR 7, SMG 7, shotgun 4, LMG 2, sniper 2, pistol 4, launcher 4 (includes the crossbow and ballistic knife) in base game data. Mod-added guns count too (e.g. MP40 Drum, Golden Python, Moon weapons). No Diamond for Specials. `camoUnlocked`/`equippedCamo`/`countCamoKill` take `weapons`. The Gunsmith shows "Gold on every assault rifle · n / m", and the toast reads "Diamond camo unlocked for every assault rifle". Placeholder texture: faceted ice-blue and white triangles with sparkles, metallic.

- **Reward camos (2026-10-04)**: a camo with `"reward": "<flag>"` in `camos.json` is not a challenge camo (it is left out of the Gold and Gunsmith counts) and unlocks when `profile.rewards[flag]` is set (camo.js `camoUnlocked`, Gunsmith shows it locked with the camo's `challenge` text until then).
