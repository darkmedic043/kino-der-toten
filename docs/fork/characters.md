# Characters, animation, first-person arms

- **Characters**: `characters.js` (registry `mods/characters/characters.json`; types `mannequin`, `gltf`, `t5`; `ThirdPersonCamera`). `mods/characters/mod.js` adds the character plus third person on T.
- **Motion model** (`motionModel()` in characters.js, shared by the procedural rig and the mannequin): `update(dt, speed, {forward, side, vy, grounded, turn})` gives a stride phase (reversed when backpedalling), run blend, strafe heading (legs swing along the movement), air/land impulses and turn lean. The pose layers are pelvis bob/sway/twist, torso counter-twist, breathing, idle head glances, knee/foot roll, arm pump, jump tuck, landing crouch and tail. The characters mod computes these from the feet delta, camera yaw rate and `player.state.grounded`.
- **Procedural rig** (`proceduralRig` in characters.js): a skinned glTF with no clips gets bones found by shape (legs = hips children going down, arms = `/arm/` chains, side from rest x, where +X is the character's left), then `aim()`s bones along character-space directions each frame. It covers idle, walk, run, a `hold()` aim pose and tail sway. For these rigs the held weapon is oriented in character space. Characters can carry a `credit` object (shown in the menu card, caption and Mods → Credits); repo-level attributions go in `CREDITS.md`.
- **Real FP arms** (`fp-arms.js`, `FirstPersonArms`; `characters.json` `fpArms: true`): a one-time rebind onto the T5 viewmodel skeleton. It clones a T5 hands model at its bind pose (`skeleton.pose()`), poses the character's arm, hand and finger bones over it (palm-length scale, direction aims, palm basis, finger chains), slides each arm so the wrists meet, then bakes the arm-weighted triangles (forearm down unless `fpArmsUpper`) into T5 bind space (`bindMatrix⁻¹`) with weights remapped to `j_shoulder/elbow/wrist/<finger>_<ri|le>_<n>`. `attach(hands)` adds a SkinnedMesh bound to each weapon rig's own skeleton and hides the T5 arms, so all animations (reload, knife, perk bottles) deform it natively. Options: `fpArmsScale`, `fpArmsTone`. The game pages pass `view, viewScene, perkDrink` to mods; the menu preview uses it too.
- **Third-person crosshair**: `ThirdPersonCamera` looks at the point where the eye camera's forward ray hits the world (min 60, max 3000), so the screen centre matches where shots land (verified within about 1 px).
- **HUD portrait**: `renderPortrait(entry,data)` in characters.js renders the character once to a 160 px PNG (head bone or top of the bounding box, 3/4 view, `portraitSpan` 0.15 × height). The characters mod shows it as `#hud-portrait` above the round panel, with a red flash when hit.
- **Third-person action layer** (characters.js `motionModel` + `actionPose`; mannequin and procedural rigs): sprung impulses `act('recoil'|'slap'|'flinch',amount,{x,z})` and eased envelopes and phases via `pose({reload,drink,swap,sprint,ads,melee,throw})`. The characters mod drives them: recoil kick per weapon (fireTime, pellets; Thundergun 2.2), reload envelope with a slap at 60%, knife phase from `meleeLeft`, grenade count drop → 0.55 s throw, weapon swap dip, drink, sprint carry, ADS, and a flinch away from the nearest zombie on `beforeDamage`.
- **Third-person held weapon**: the characters mod attaches the current weapon's `worldModel` to `character.hand` (mannequin right wrist, T5 `j_wrist_ri`, glTF regex or `handBone`), undoing the hand's scale; `entry.weapon` adjusts the grip. The mannequin has a two-handed aim pose via `hold()`.
- **FP arms fitting (2026-09-29)**: per-joint offsets. Every mapped joint (shoulder, elbow, wrist, each finger knuckle) moves onto its T5 joint and the mesh stretches between them, by skin weights; unmapped bones follow the nearest mapped ancestor. So paws with different proportions close on the grips. The kept geometry is the forearm, hand and the elbow half of the upper arm (t > .5 along shoulder→elbow), because forearm-only showed a severed end on knife swings and the full upper arm brought the Protogen's shoulder armour into view. `fpArmsUpper: true` keeps it all; `false` gives forearm only. Viewmodel camera FOV is 51 (BO1 cg_fov 65 on 4:3); at 60 the rear faces of viewmodels showed stretched.
- **First-person arms per character**: `characters.json` `hands` (`pow`/`usmc`/`pressure_suit` or a T5-rigged path) plus `handsTint`/`handsFlat`; `armsUrl`/`tintArms` in characters.js (tints the shared cached materials and keeps the originals). The characters mod swaps `data.characters.viewmodel_usa_pow_arms` in `prepare()`. The menu's Character tab has a Body / First person preview (a `ViewWeapon` holding the loadout primary).
