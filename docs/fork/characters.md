# Characters, animation, first-person arms

- **Characters**: `characters.js` (registry `mods/characters/characters.json`; types `mannequin`, `gltf`, `t5`; `ThirdPersonCamera`). `mods/characters/mod.js` adds the character plus third person on T.
- **Motion model** (`motionModel()` in characters.js, shared by the procedural rig and the mannequin): `update(dt, speed, {forward, side, vy, grounded, turn})` gives a stride phase (reversed when backpedalling), run blend, strafe heading (legs swing along the movement), air/land impulses and turn lean. The pose layers are pelvis bob/sway/twist, torso counter-twist, breathing, idle head glances, knee/foot roll, arm pump, jump tuck, landing crouch and tail. The characters mod computes these from the feet delta, camera yaw rate and `player.state.grounded`.
- **Airborne filter (characters mod)**: stairs and small bumps drop `grounded` for a few frames; the rigs only get `grounded:false` (and the real vy) after a jump (`movement.state.jumped`) or 0.25 s in the air, so stepping down no longer plays the jump/fall/land poses.
- **BO1 player animations (2026-10-02, `"motion": "bo1"`; Bondrewd, Protogen)**: `bo1-motion.js` `bo1Rig`. A hidden T5 body (`c_ger_honorguard_body1`, or `motionBody`) plays BO1's `pb_*` player-body clips (`.tools/build_player_anims.sh` → `mods/characters/animations/`, git-ignored; missing clips fall back to the procedural rig). The character's limbs are first posed over the T5 bind (arms, hands by palm basis, fingers, legs, feet), then each frame every mapped bone gets the T5 joint's world rotation delta since bind (spine chains of any length map to T5's ends; the pelvis also copies the T5 pelvis travel, scaled by hip height). Gotcha: BO1's player rig hangs the spine off `torso_stabilizer`, which the clips counter-rotate against `j_mainroot`; the zombie bodies lack it, so the rig inserts it (without it the body folded over backwards). States: idle/ADS, walk (`stand_shoot_walk`) and run (`combatrun`) in four directions blended by movement angle and speed, sprint, crouch (+ADS, crouch run ×4), prone + crawl ×4, slide (`pb_terrain_slide`), dive + dive land, jump takeoff (held while airborne) and landings, mantle (`pb_climbup`). Loops in a set share one phase so the directions stay in step; playback rate follows speed (`NOMINAL`). Weights ease and are normalised. Actions (recoil, reload, knife, throw, drink, swap, flinch) and camera pitch are additive rotations on the spine, head and arms, carried down the subtree. The held gun rides `tag_weapon_right`, moved from T5's wrist onto the character's hand (`hand` = `bo1_grip`). `applyStance` skips its whole-body tilt for these rigs (`stanceAnimated`); the characters mod now also passes `crouch` and `pitch`, and sets `kino.character` (debug handle).
- **Spring bones (2026-10-02, characters.json `springBones`; Bondrewd's coat)**: `spring-bones.js` `SpringBones`, run by `createCharacter` after any rig poses the body (`withSprings`). Each listed chain root and its descendants (`childMatch` picks the child when a bone branches, e.g. pockets) are simulated in world space at a fixed 60 Hz: Verlet inertia less `drag`, a pull of `stiffness` back to the animated pose, `gravity` (×9.8 m/s² in model units), capsule colliders along bones (`colliders` [{bone, to, radius}]), the bone length kept and a `maxAngle` cone from the animated direction. Resets on teleports. Coat tuning history: physbone-like values flung it; stiffness .22/drag .3/follow .85 was "wobbly and stiff" (an underdamped spring: strong pull, little damping); now stiffness .025, drag .16, gravity .8, maxAngle 50, follow .6 — the back tail trails ~12 units behind at a run and on stopping swings forward, sways back once and settles in ~0.5 s (user wanted it to act like a coat). Then "goes way too up too often": `followY` 1 (vertical body motion — stairs, landings — is carried fully, never flung), gravity 2, maxAngle 25, leg capsules .09/.08: tails rise at most 6.5-12.5 units running down stairs (was 18-24). `follow` (default .85) carries the simulated points along with the chain's anchor bone each step (its world-matrix delta), so only part of the body's own movement and turning becomes inertia; at 0 (full world-space inertia) snap mouse turns and sprint starts flung the coat its whole length (tips moved 34-37 units in a scripted run/sprint/snap-turn/stop test; with follow .85, stiffness .22, drag .3, maxAngle 35 they move 6-17). Bondrewd's gravity and leg capsules follow the avatar's VRChat `PB_Coat` physbone (pull .2, spring .5, stiffness .2, gravity .5, 60° limit), but stiffer (user: the physbone-like values flung it all over) and its four leg capsules, converted to GLB units (prefab ×~65): 8 chains (Back, Front, Split and Side, left and right).
- **Procedural rig** (`proceduralRig` in characters.js): a skinned glTF with no clips gets bones found by shape (legs = hips children going down, arms = `/arm/` chains, side from rest x, where +X is the character's left), then `aim()`s bones along character-space directions each frame. It covers idle, walk, run, a `hold()` aim pose and tail sway. For these rigs the held weapon is oriented in character space. Characters can carry a `credit` object (shown in the menu card, caption and Mods → Credits); repo-level attributions go in `CREDITS.md`.
- **Real FP arms** (`fp-arms.js`, `FirstPersonArms`; `characters.json` `fpArms: true`): a one-time rebind onto the T5 viewmodel skeleton. It clones a T5 hands model at its bind pose (`skeleton.pose()`), poses the character's arm, hand and finger bones over it (palm-length scale, direction aims, palm basis, finger chains), slides each arm so the wrists meet, then bakes the arm-weighted triangles (forearm down unless `fpArmsUpper`) into T5 bind space (`bindMatrix⁻¹`) with weights remapped to `j_shoulder/elbow/wrist/<finger>_<ri|le>_<n>`. `attach(hands)` adds a SkinnedMesh bound to each weapon rig's own skeleton and hides the T5 arms, so all animations (reload, knife, perk bottles) deform it natively. Options: `fpArmsScale`, `fpArmsTone`. The game pages pass `view, viewScene, perkDrink` to mods; the menu preview uses it too.
- **Third-person crosshair**: `ThirdPersonCamera` looks at the point where the eye camera's forward ray hits the world (min 60, max 3000), so the screen centre matches where shots land (verified within about 1 px).
- **HUD portrait**: `renderPortrait(entry,data)` in characters.js renders the character once to a 160 px PNG (head bone or top of the bounding box, 3/4 view, `portraitSpan` 0.15 × height). The characters mod shows it as `#hud-portrait` above the round panel, with a red flash when hit.
- **Third-person action layer** (characters.js `motionModel` + `actionPose`; mannequin and procedural rigs): sprung impulses `act('recoil'|'slap'|'flinch',amount,{x,z})` and eased envelopes and phases via `pose({reload,drink,swap,sprint,ads,melee,throw})`. The characters mod drives them: recoil kick per weapon (fireTime, pellets; Thundergun 2.2), reload envelope with a slap at 60%, knife phase from `meleeLeft`, grenade count drop → 0.55 s throw, weapon swap dip, drink, sprint carry, ADS, and a flinch away from the nearest zombie on `beforeDamage`.
- **Third-person held weapon**: the characters mod attaches the current weapon's `worldModel` to `character.hand` (mannequin right wrist, T5 `j_wrist_ri`, glTF regex or `handBone`), undoing the hand's scale; `entry.weapon` adjusts the grip. The mannequin has a two-handed aim pose via `hold()`.
- **FP arms fitting (2026-09-29)**: per-joint offsets. Every mapped joint (shoulder, elbow, wrist, each finger knuckle) moves onto its T5 joint and the mesh stretches between them, by skin weights; unmapped bones follow the nearest mapped ancestor. So paws with different proportions close on the grips. The kept geometry is the forearm, hand and the elbow half of the upper arm (t > .5 along shoulder→elbow), because forearm-only showed a severed end on knife swings and the full upper arm brought the Protogen's shoulder armour into view. `fpArmsUpper: true` keeps it all; `false` gives forearm only. Viewmodel camera FOV is 51 (BO1 cg_fov 65 on 4:3); at 60 the rear faces of viewmodels showed stretched.
- **First-person arms per character**: `characters.json` `hands` (`pow`/`usmc`/`pressure_suit` or a T5-rigged path) plus `handsTint`/`handsFlat`; `armsUrl`/`tintArms` in characters.js (tints the shared cached materials and keeps the originals). The characters mod swaps `data.characters.viewmodel_usa_pow_arms` in `prepare()`. The menu's Character tab has a Body / First person preview (a `ViewWeapon` holding the loadout primary).
- **Blockbench / Cobblemon operators (2026-10-01)**: `.tools/bbmodel_to_glb.py model.bbmodel anims.json out.glb idle=… walk=… run=…@speed` converts a Bedrock-format Blockbench model: each group becomes a node holding its boxes (rigid; Euler ZYX about `origin`; per-face UVs; nearest-filtered texture, alpha mask), and Bedrock animations (Molang: `math.*` in degrees, `query.anim_time`; or keyframes) are sampled at 30 fps into looping clips named idle/walk/run (the gltf type picks clips by name). Bedrock rotations apply as (−x, −y, z) and positions as (−x, y, z) on the rest pose; the loop length comes from `animation_length` or the `* 90 * k` frequency. Cobblemon models face −Z, so `yaw: 180`. **Zoroark** (`.tools/build_zoroark.sh`, CC BY-NC 3.0 the Cobblemon team; Pokémon IP) is git-ignored; its registry entry is `optional`, and `loadCharacterRegistry` drops optional entries whose model file is missing. Hand: `locator_hand_primary`.
- **Zoroark hands and posture (2026-10-01)**: the converter now writes a **skinned** mesh (every group a joint, boxes bound 100% to their group), so `FirstPersonArms` can use it. Options: `split=group:joint:margin` binds a group's vertices past a child joint to that joint (Zoroark's paw end of `arm_*2` → `locator_hand_*`, a wrist), `reparent=a,b:parent` moves groups under another joint keeping their world pose (the claws onto the hand), `pose=bone:x,y,z` adds degrees to a rest rotation under the animations (Zoroark stood upright, head levelled, legs straightened under the hips, arms down at the sides; mind the sides: on the +x (right) arm a negative z lowers it, on −x a positive z), `shrink=bone:factor` scales a subtree about its pivot and `offset=bone:x,y,z` moves it, `extend=group:x0,y0,z0,x1,y1,z1` grows a group's own boxes (Zoroark's `hair3` lengthened 10 back and 6 down to bridge the gap to the moved mane). Zoroark's mane: forelock left as modelled, the chain bent down at `hair4` (behind the head, not at `hair`, which is at the front of the head and dragged the blocks through the skull), shrunk to 0.7, pushed back (offset 0,-2,9; bent 60°) off the body, the lower end swept back into a tail. Optional models get `?v=<mtime>` on their URL (binaries are cached a day, so a rebuilt model was served stale). New `characters.json` options read by fp-arms.js: `fpArmsArm` (regex for the upper-arm bones), `fpArmsFingers` ({index,mid,pinky…: regex}; Zoroark's claws), `fpArmsFit: 'arm'` (size by shoulder→wrist length; paw claws sit at the wrist, so palm sizing blew the arms up ~2.5×), `fpArmsExclude` (regex, bones left out of first person; Zoroark's arm fur), `fpArmsScale`.
- **Bondrewd (2026-10-01)**: Bunny Squad's VRChat model (Malganis Lefay; readme: "feel free to use, edit, and share"; Made in Abyss fan model) from the user's downloaded `.unitypackage` (a gzipped tar: one folder per asset with `pathname` + `asset`). `.tools/build_bondrewd.sh <package>` unpacks it, scales the 4K textures (diffuse 2048, normals 1024), and runs `.tools/build_bondrewd_blend.py` in headless Blender: FBX import, the three materials rebuilt from the package textures (the FBX names files the package doesn't contain: `Science_dif.png` etc.), GLB export (skinned, no shape keys/animations, WebP) — 2.7 MB. VRChat humanoid bone names, so the procedural rig and `fpArms` work without options. Git-ignored, `optional` in the registry, height 80.
- **Operators and skins (2026-10-02, Black Ops 7 style)**: Armory > Operator shows operator tiles (portraits from `renderPortrait`, queued and cached per entry) and, under the stage, a SKINS strip for the selected operator. A skin is a full characters.json entry with `"skinOf": "<operator id>"` and `skinName`; the operator's own entry is its default skin (`skinName` shown, "DEFAULT"). The equipped skin is `profile.character` (so the game needs nothing new); `profile.skins[operator]` remembers the last skin per operator. Each skin has its own `variants`/`variantBase` and picks (`profile.variants[skin id]`). Portraits frame by the entry's `height` (a raised tail blew up the bounding box).
- **Bondrewd Premium skin (2026-10-02)**: from the user's "Bunny Squad Bondrewd Premium and Quest" package (PC model only; the Quest Combat/Blessed meshes are low-poly versions of the same looks). `.tools/build_bondrewd_premium.sh` → `models/bondrewd_premium/` (GLB + swap textures, git-ignored). Its Poiyomi toggles were rebuilt: shape keys (outfit ScienceSuit/Boots_Combat, gauntlets Gauntlet_Off/Curse_Dart_Launcher/unHide_Sparagmus, pockets, boots incl. Boots_Combat_Manga via `when`, paper eyes + faces + noodles, mask damage/hollow, Blessing claws/Puff/Fluff_Out, TailCut, coat, bulky); texture swaps (Combat outfit = combat_dif/norm on Body, Anime relic eye); clip masks (Metal_Alpha on gauntlets/tank, inverted for Brick; Transform's fur mask, removed for Fur/Blessed; tail tip for Cut). Gotchas: every diffuse has a cutout alpha (the build marks materials MASK at the avatar cutoffs .4/.475/.5, else coat lining shows white); the helmet lives on the Transform mesh; three renames the Tank mesh `Tank_1` (a bone is also "Tank"), so mesh keys match with the `_N` suffix stripped; the tail toggle's values are absolute bends from a straight chain (the anim's first key isn't the rest), positive about local X (S-curve up behind the head), root scale 0.1 → 1; Eye Split rotations are Unity ZXY eulers turned into local deltas with (x,−y,−z,w). Variants apply after the height fit (a raised tail shrank him). Tail is a second spring group (gravity 0). Toggles that only drive particles, sounds, props (Farcaress/Shaker/Sparagmus armaments, cartridge yeet) or emotes were left out.
- **Premium follow-ups (2026-10-02)**: the tail uses spring-bones `"mode": "drag"` (a heavy rope: each joint trails the one before it at bone length, sinks at `sag` m/s and rests on the floor (`floor`, the holder's height, with friction); no stiffness or inertia, so it can't flail; the springy version flung a 25-joint, 4 m tail everywhere) and is posed drooping (root −70°, first 7 segments +10°). First-person fur rendered black: the GLB carried five Poiyomi colour sets, three's material expected `COLOR_0`, and the FP bake doesn't copy colours; the build now strips extra UV/colour sets (8.5 → 5 MB) and fp-arms always sets `vertexColors=false`. Claws: Blessed must un-shrink claws only on the Transform mesh (`"Transform/ClawShrinkL": 0`; the Body keeps ClawShrink 1, as in the avatar), hence mesh-prefixed morph keys. Retarget FP bones now carry the source bone names (debugging).
- **Spring chains interact with the avatar and the level (2026-10-02)**: colliders are capped per joint at that joint's rest distance (`reach`, computed on reset), so body capsules (Bondrewd: Hips→Spine, Spine→Chest .22, thighs .12, shins .1) stop the coat sinking into him but never push it out of its rest shape (0% of coat points inside the body in a running test). `floor` now asks `spring.ground(x,y,z)` (set by the characters mod: a downward world raycast from 40 above) for the ground under each point, falling back to the feet plane (menu): the dragging tail drapes down stairs (tested on the lobby stairs: each segment 2 units above the step under it, heights 152→117), and the coat rests on the ground when crouching or prone. `createCharacter` exposes `character.springs`.
- **Coat collider fix (2026-10-02)**: three's GLTFLoader sanitises node names ("Left leg" → "Left_leg"), so spring-bone lookups by the original names silently dropped every leg collider; `byName` now tries both. Collisions now run after the swing limit (which used to pull panels back into a swinging leg) and also test segment midpoints; `drive` turns each coat chain's rest shape by part of its nearest thigh's swing (front .7, side .45, split .35, back .25). Coat vertices inside a leg while sprinting: 0.29% (0.53% without the drive). Bondrewd's materials are double-sided (Poiyomi `_Cull 0`; both build scripts patch `doubleSided`): fur and claw cards vanished from behind on knife swings.
- **Variant engine**: options can set `morphs` (plain or `Mesh/key`), `bones` ({scale, rot° delta, pos delta}), `meshes` (visibility), `surfaces` (per mesh or material: map/normalMap/emissiveMap/alphaMap files next to the model, alphaTest) and `when` ({"group:option": extra}); `variantBase` is applied first. Bone-pose groups rebuild the menu preview (the rig and springs capture the rest pose).
- **Operator customisation (2026-10-02, characters.json `variants`)**: groups of options; each option sets morph targets by name (`morphs`), unnamed targets go to 0, a missing pick uses the option with `default` or the first. Picks live in the shared profile as `profile.variants[characterId] = {group: option}` (Armory > Operator "CUSTOMISE" chips, `renderVariants` in home.js; applied live to the preview). `applyVariants(root, entry, entry.choice)` in characters.js runs on every `createCharacter` clone and in `FirstPersonArms.build` (three's `getVertexPosition` applies morphs, so the FP bake picks them up); the characters mod sets `entry.choice` from the profile. Bondrewd's groups mirror the original avatar's VRChat menu (Gauntlet Base/Brick, Paper Eyes, Science Pockets Anime/Manga, Manga Boots) plus the paper-eye expressions (they only redraw the paper notes), Long coat and Bulky; `.tools/build_bondrewd_blend.py` keeps only those shape keys (`KEEP`) and exports morphs without normals (+190 KB). Original default is Gauntlet Base (`Gauntlet_Off` 1); the GLB basis is Brick. Mug/Plate keys were skipped (need the avatar's physbones).
- **FP arms retarget mode (2026-10-02, `fpArmsRetarget: true`, used by Bondrewd)**: instead of re-skinning onto T5 bones, the arms keep the character's own skeleton and proportions. Each frame (`onBeforeRender`, after the scene's matrices are final) the hand is pinned to T5's wrist with T5's wrist rotation, the forearm and upper arm take T5's elbow/shoulder rotation and hang back from their child joint at their own length, fingers take T5's finger rotations at their own segment lengths, and other bones follow their parent. Bones are free-standing `THREE.Bone`s with `matrixWorld` set directly (bind matrix identity, vertices in the posed bind space plus the wrist shift). Why: the per-joint offset method pulled Bondrewd's short forearm out to T5's length (a thin stretched sleeve between the rigid bracer and the hand) and noodled his short fingers; the user wanted the hands on model. Fingers then do not wrap grips exactly, which reads fine. The finger options below only apply to the default (offset) mode.
- **FP arms per-finger options (2026-10-01)**: `fpArmsFingerScale` ({thumb:.6}) scales a finger's geometry toward its base knuckle before baking, and binds it **rigidly to the base knuckle** (Bondrewd's gauntlet thumb plate is long and stuck out across the pistol slide; scaled but still bent at T5's thumb joints it folded into broken-looking pieces); `fpArmsKeepFingers` (['thumb']) pins only the base knuckle so a finger keeps its own segment lengths. `fpArmsFingerFit` (true) scales each articulated finger uniformly (length and thickness, about its base knuckle, weighted by skin weight) so knuckle 0 to 2 matches T5's length: Bondrewd's index is ~1.7x shorter per segment than T5's, and stretched only lengthwise it became a thin noodle looping round the pistol grip. All characters: finger offsets now blend along each segment (knuckle to knuckle) instead of each segment moving rigidly, so any leftover stretch spreads along the finger rather than crowding the weight-blend band at the joint. Debug: the characters mod sets `kino.view` (the first-person ViewWeapon). To inspect FP arms: stop `view.rig.update`/`view.update`, then turn `view.pivot` and colour `fp_arms` vertices by dominant T5 bone.
- **Retargeted hands rolled (fixed 2026-10-03)**: on base Bondrewd the hands looked inverted on guns (palm normal 68°/110° off T5's). Two causes in `fp-arms.js`: `handBasis` built a left-handed basis (side = up × f), a reflection that `setFromRotationMatrix` turns into a wrong quaternion, so the bind-pose hand fit was off; and the retarget anchor followed only the wrist bone while T5's animations move the knuckles relative to it. Now `handBasis` uses side = f × up, and the retarget hand turns by T5's live knuckle frame (mid/index/pinky `_0`) since bind, pinned to T5's wrist (falls back to the wrist bone if a rig lacks the knuckles). Measured: finger direction, palm side and normal all ≥0.99 against T5. Zoroark's offset-mode arms improved too (a stray claw block is gone).
