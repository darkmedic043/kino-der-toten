# Credits and the Shop (roadmap Phase 6, 2026-10-14)

A play-earned currency and a cosmetic-only Shop on its own page. PDF open questions answered with its suggestions: credits come from a mix (match payout, challenges, milestones, prestige); the shop is cosmetic only (no XP boosts). All numbers are placeholders in `mods/credits/credits.json` and `mods/shop/catalog.json`.

## Credits (`mods/credits`)
- **Store** `kino.mods.credits` = `{balance, ledger (last 50 changes), challenges:{daily, weekly}, milestones:{map:{round:true}}}` (cloud.js TRACKED). `credits.js` is shared (menu, Shop page, game): `change`/`grantCredits`, `currentChallenges`, `countChallenges`, `matchPayout`. Saves fire a `kino-credits` window event (the menu's chip updates).
- **Earning** (made sparser the same day, user: "a bit harder to get": about half): match payout on the game-over screen (8 per round past 5 + 0.25 per kill, cap 200, ~94 for a round-12 match; appended to `#results`); first time reaching rounds 10/20/30/50 on a map 50/125/250/500 (announced when it happens); weapon prestige I/II/Master 150/250/500 and player prestige 500 (home.js prestige handlers); 3 daily (40 each, reset at local midnight) and 3 weekly (120 each, reset Monday) challenges, rolled from the pool with the date as the seed (every device agrees), weekly goals ×4. Stats counted in game (`mods/credits/mod.js`): kills, headshots, pistol headshots, melee, explosive kills, points, rounds survived, best round, gums chewed (new `gumChewed` event in the gobblegums mod), ability uses (`abilityUsed`), ammo mod procs (`ammoModFired`), perks drunk, Mystery Box guns (wraps `mysteryBox.take`), Pack-a-Punches; flushed 2 s after the last change, on game over and on unload.
- **No credits** when `window.kino.cheats.used` (verified: a cheat game left the balance untouched) or on `noPayoutMaps` (empty since 2026-10-16: the user asked for Sandbox games to pay out and count for challenges). A local balance can be edited; accepted for a local fork, the ledger shows odd jumps.

## The Shop (a main-menu tab: `mods/shop/shop-tab.js` mounted into `#shop-root`, styles `shop.css`; rules `mods/shop/shop.js`; data `mods/shop/catalog.json`)
- **Now a tab** (same day, user: "shop should also work like other tabs… the side bar should still be visible"): the SHOP button in the sidebar (with the balance chip) shows `#tab-shop`; `mountShop` builds it once and refreshes on later visits; its views (Featured, categories, Credits) are internal, not hash routes. `shop.html` only redirects to `./#shop`. The camo drawer uses the shared `mods/weapon-levels/gun-preview.js`.
- **Store** `kino.mods.shop` = `{owned, equip:{reticle, hitmarker, kill, hud, menu, frame, sound, glow, card}, seen}` (TRACKED). Camos are equipped per gun in the Gunsmith (`profile.camo`) and outfits are characters (Operator pane), so they have no slot here.
- **Layout**: Balance and rotation timers on top; FEATURED (3 daily items and the weekly bundle of 3 at 30% off, both seeded by the date from the whole catalogue), seven category views, CREDITS (challenges with progress and reset timers, how to earn, the ledger). Drawer: preview (camos on a turning 3D M16 with the animated camo clock, outfits as a rendered portrait, reticles/hitmarkers on a mock, cards as SVG, sound sets play a synthesized sample), BUY (two clicks), EQUIP. Items marked `rotating` only show in the daily slots or the bundle until owned. NEW badges from `seen`. Phones (≤760 px, or a landscape phone): a category dropdown and the drawer as a bottom sheet (`top:auto`, or it stretched from the top).
- **Reward registry** `window.kino.rewards.grant(type, id)` (`grantReward`: `item`, `credits`; installed on the menu, the Shop page and in game) is the one path for purchases and, in Phase 7, the pass.
- **Catalogue (50 items)**: 10 camos (camos.json entries with `"shop": <item>`, procedural art in camo.js `SHOP_DRAW`: Ember, Aurora, Frostbite, Circuit, Digital Rain, Chrome and Brushed Steel (metallic), Hazard, and animated Spectrum and Pulse through the Master camo's anim/glow path; owned ones are unlocked on every gun in `camoUnlocked` and listed in a new Gunsmith SHOP tab; challenge counts skip them); 5 outfits (Test Subject recolours in characters.json with `"shop"`; `loadCharacterRegistry` lists them once owned, so the Operator pane, portraits and arms just work); reticles (crosshair glyph and colour, the red dot takes the colour), hitmarkers, kill colours (the engine's default blood `effect` recoloured); HUD themes (points / round / ammo ring colours for hud-bo7), menu accents (`--red-hi`), portrait frames; hit-sound sets (`window.kino.hitSoundSet` reshapes the hud mod's thumps); Mystery Box glow (`window.kino.boxGlow`: the lighting mod's map bulb emissive, its lamp colour and a now-white halo tinted at runtime, no rebake); calling cards (`mods/shop/card.js` SVG: rank card in the menu, the pause menu under the map title, behind your row in the co-op lobby via the lobby info `cd`). Music packs need the user's own music, so none ship.
- **Recorded hit sounds** (2026-11-07, the user's uploads): a `sound` item whose `data.hits` has `samples` (file names in `mods/shop/sounds/`, `volume`) plays a recorded sample per hit instead of the synthesized thump (hud mod `sample()`): a random one when there are several, never the same twice running (Zap has 3), heads at 1.08x speed, a new hit fades the last one out in ~10 ms, kills keep the deep synth thud. The shop mod resolves the URLs into `kino.hitSoundSet.samples`; the hud mod preloads them at setup and on `start`, and plays the thump until they've decoded or if they fail. The shop drawer's PLAY plays each sample, then a headshot. Items `pass.s2.sound.zap`/`classic`/`modern` (Season 1 pages 2/5/7). The files are git-ignored (`mods/shop/sounds/`) and not installed: the zap is a "free sound effect" download and the other two are of unknown origin, so they're streamed: Rind fetches every file its install lacks from the site (`~/projects/rind/src/install.js`), as it does the BO1/BO3 assets and the s2 camos/cards, so Rind players hear them too (checked: the public URL serves them, 2026-11-07). Cut from the upload with ffmpeg (mono 44.1 kHz WAV, 3 ms fade in / 20 ms out); the zip is in `~/helm-uploads/keep/20261107-052203-s1.zip`.
- **Image calling cards** (2026-11-06, user: "here are 8 calling cards"): a card's `data.image` (a `/mods/...` path; anything else is ignored, since the co-op lobby passes the card data between players as `cd`, which now carries `image`) is drawn over the card with `preserveAspectRatio="xMidYMid slice"` (CoD cards are 4:1, ours 300×64: a sliver is cropped top and bottom), a dark fade on the left and an outlined (not blurred) name. Animated WebP plays by itself, in every place a card shows. `c1` is the fallback background when the image is missing (Rind installs: the Season 1 cards in `mods/shop/cards/s2/` are third-party and git-ignored). Check: `lowpri node scratch/cards-s1/sheet.mjs`.
- **In game** (`mods/shop/mod.js`): applies the equipped cosmetics at load and on a new game.
- Verified: bought 4 items from 5,000 (→ 2,400), all still owned and equipped after a reload; the Chrome camo unlocked on every gun; in a match the reticle, box glow and pause card were on; a round-11 match paid +110 (6×15 + 40×0.5) plus the round-10 milestone 100, both in the ledger; challenge progress counted; phones: no sideways overflow, tap-to-buy works, the drawer is a bottom sheet; the menu shows the chip, the card, the gold accent and the owned outfit.
- Not done: the co-op lobby card is wired but wasn't seen with two players; seasonal sets wait for Phase 7's seasons.

## Kill effects and titles (2026-11-08, user: "i like the kill effect and titles, can you implements those?")

Two new equip slots, both Season 1 Melon Pass rewards (`pass:"s2"`, so they show in the Shop only once owned). `slotOf` (shop.js): a `reticle` item with `data.killfx` fills slot `killfx`; a `card` item with `data.title` fills slot `title` (the categories are now "Reticles, hits & kill effects" and "Calling cards & titles").

- **Kill effects** (`mods/shop/kill-fx.js`, installed by shop/mod.js): `pass.s2.killfx.{payout,swarm,grounded,tolled}`, `data.killfx` = the id. On your own `kill` events (co-op `remote` kills are skipped, and other players don't see yours: the effect is local only) it plays at the body, 44 up (18 for dogs), at most ~4 a second in a crowd. Drawn with the fx mod's pool through `window.kino.fx` (`emit`, `bolt`, `flash`, `jitter`, `addTexture(key, texture, grid)`; fx/mod.js gained `wobble` for jittery flight and a warmed ground-bolt mesh, so the first strike compiles nothing). Payout: a gold glow and 14 flipping coins (canvas atlas `kfx_coin`, 4 cells) with gravity, a clink. Swarm: an amber puff and 11 bees (`kfx_bee`, 2 wing cells) that wobble upward, a buzz. Grounded: a ground bolt, a blue light flash, sparks, dark ash and blue embers, a crackle. Tolled: two pale rings, grey puffs and a bell with inharmonic partials. Sounds are tiny Web Audio synths through the game's master gain (volume settings apply). Test hook: `kino.killFx.play(id, position)`.
- **Titles**: `pass.s2.title.{debtcollector,beekeeper,apex,lasttoll}`, `data.title` = the text. Shown before the level on the calling card (menu `#p-card`, pause card: `DEBT COLLECTOR · LEVEL 12`), alone in gold when no card is on, under your name on the scoreboard (Tab), and under each player's name in the co-op lobby. Co-op sends the title's **item id** (`tt` in the lobby info), never text: `window.kino.titleOf(id)` looks it up in the catalogue, so a peer can't inject a string. `window.kino.coopInfo(peerId)` (multiplayer mod) hands the scoreboard peers' lobby info.
- **Menu art** (`mods/shop/cosmetic-art.js`, no three.js): `killFxSvg(id)`, a looping SMIL picture of a zombie bust and the effect (no blur filters), and `titlePlate(title)`, a nameplate; thumbs.js uses them for the Shop grid, the drawer and the pass tab.
- Verified (`scratch/killfx/game.mjs`, Sandbox on the GPU): each effect plays (frames checked), a local kill event emitted 13 sprites and a remote one none, the title showed on the home card, the pause card and the scoreboard, `titleOf` rejects unknown ids, no page errors; renderer 1.2-1.4 GB, flat across the effects.

## Emblems (2026-11-08, user: "here are 9 emblems, all the pngs are animated pngs if you can convert them to work in here")

A third new slot, `emblem` (`slotOf`: `cat:"emblem"`). Nine Season 1 pass items `pass.s2.emblem.<id>`, `data.emblem` = `/mods/shop/emblems/s2/<id>.webp`: Blind Rage, Bulldawg, Evidence, Mine, Warrior Wasp (static, rare) and Death Wave, Marrowed, No Reception, Renegade Shock (animated, legendary).

- **Files**: the user's pack is BO6/BO7 art, so `mods/shop/emblems/s2/` is git-ignored and never matches a Rind OWN rule (like the s2 cards and camos). The five static ones are the pack's own 100 px WebPs, copied as they are (a lossless re-encode doubled them). The four animated ones came as APNGs (1.5-6 MB, 16 frames); `scratch/emblems-tool/convert.py` (Pillow through `uv run --with pillow`, no Pillow on the host; ffmpeg can't read the APNGs' frames properly and can't decode animated WebP) wrote 256 px animated WebP, lossy quality 88, method 6, frame timings kept: 230-535 KB each, 1.8 MB for all nine. Chrome plays an animated WebP inside an inline SVG `<image>` too.
- **Shown**: a 54x54 badge (on a 35% black square) at the left end of the calling card, `cardSvg(d,title,sub,emblem)` (card.js), the text moves right to x=66; home `#p-card` and the pause card. An emblem with no card goes on `PLAIN_CARD` (card.js, dark). It replaces the avatar square on the scoreboard (Tab; `img.em`, contain, no grey filter) and in the co-op lobby (`.av img.em`).
- **Co-op**: the lobby info carries the emblem's **item id** (`em`), resolved by `window.kino.emblemOf(id)` (shop/mod.js) to the catalogue's path, which must pass `safePath` (card.js: only `/mods/...`, no `..`). A peer can't send a URL. Image calling cards' `data.image` goes through `safePath` too now.
- Verified (`scratch/emblems/game.mjs` on the GPU, `anim.mjs`): the badge on the home card, the pause card and the scoreboard, all nine Shop thumbs load, `emblemOf` rejects a title id and junk, 4 distinct frames from 4 shots of an animated card, no page errors; renderer 1.3-1.45 GB.
