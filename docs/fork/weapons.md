# Weapons: Moon, Thundergun, animation fix, ammo bulbs

- **Moon wonder weapons mod** (`mods/moon-weapons`): `prepare()` copies `microwavegundw_zm`, `microwavegun_zm` and `minigun_zm` from `moon/combat-data.json` (asset paths are already web-root relative) with projectileSpeed and explosionRadius set to 0 (hit-scan), and adds the Wave Gun and Death Machine to `boxPool`. `setup()` merges the Moon `wpn/microwave` and `lmg/minigun` audio cues, wraps `audio.weapon`, makes Zap Gun bullet hits lethal via `beforeEnemyDamage`, runs the Wave Gun cone kill (range 1000/1400, dot > 0.78) when `session.shots` increments, and B swaps dw ↔ combined (separate ammo stored on `weapon.other`; the first swap gives the other form the same share of its ammo, and a wrapped `session.powerup('full_ammo')` refills the stashed form too).
- **Thundergun mod** (`mods/thundergun`): the exported Thundergun glTFs lost their alpha modes, so the mod patches the shared cached materials once (loaded via `loadModel`, so the viewmodel, box display and third-person hold are all fixed): alphaTest 0.5 only on the wire mesh (the body texture's alpha is not a cut-out mask: cutting it removed the dashed ammo ring and ribbed strips); `chamber_glass` and the lens become transparent; `chamber_main` becomes an additive, emissive, scrolling energy texture that flares on each shot. It wraps `audio.weapon` ('shot' + base id `thundergun_zm`) to spawn a shockwave in the world scene (streaked additive cone, three expanding rings, muzzle flash sprite, dust points, and a screen flash overlay) sized to the game's 600-unit, dot > 0.65 kill cone. It tags `cause==='thunder'` victims in `beforeEnemyDamage` and throws their corpses on `kill` (ballistic arc, wall and ground raycasts, backward tilt); skipped for `host.remoteDamage`.
- **Weapon-part animation fix** (upstream `animation.js` edit): T5 viewmodel xanim *translations* on weapon-model bones (bolts, magazines, pumps, straps, the Thundergun's `tag_pump` → rig → `tag_clip` drum, the Zap Gun/Wave Gun parts) are displacements from the bone's bind position, while rotations are absolute. `makeClip` used to apply them as absolute, which pulled parts into the receiver: the Thundergun's drum (its magazine) vanished into the body and its barrel floated detached, the reload barely moved, and the MP40 magazine sat about 4 units off. `ViewWeapon.equip` now stores `userData.bindPosition` on every gun and left-gun bone except `j_gun`/`j_gun1` (anchors, or animated perk bottles), and `makeClip` adds it to position keys. Hands and locomotion clips are unchanged. Evidence: 1,848 gun position tracks look like pure single-axis offsets (e.g. M1911 empty slide −2.16,0,0; Ithaca pump 4.06,0,0); rotation tracks mostly match the bind pose (absolute).
- **Thundergun ammo bulbs** (`mods/ammo-counters`): the original showed rounds in the magazine as red lights on the drum (FX on `tag_bulb1-8`, not exported). The mod wraps `view.equip`, adds an additive red sprite to each `tag_bulb` bone of the Thundergun viewmodel, pushes each sprite 0.9 out along the gun’s −X (the tags sit 0.15 behind the drum face, so the glow was buried), and lights bulb n while `mag ≥ n` (2 normally; Pack-a-Punch 4, but the model has no `tag_bulb4`, so 3 show, as in the original). The Zap Gun's "87" is a printed decal in the original, not a counter.
- **Zap Gun ↔ Wave Gun transitions** (moon-weapons mod): the Zap Gun rig's `altDropAnim` (dw_2_combo, 1.73 s, with `wpn_micro_rld_join`) plays before swapping to the Wave Gun; splitting equips the Zap Guns and plays `altRaiseAnim` (combo_2_dw, 1.33 s, with `wpn_micro_rld_separate`). `equipInPlace` skips the normal raise, firing is locked via `session.fireLeft`, and the other form's model and animations are preloaded while either is held.
- **Thundergun reload sounds** (thundergun mod): `notetrackSounds` maps `sndnt#fly_thundergun_cell_lock` to `fly_thundergun_cell_slide_lock`, and the mod plays `fly_thundergun_eject` at 6% and the charging beep `fly_thundergun_cell_on` at 76% of the reload (both have no animation cue).
- **Gersh Device and QED** (`mods/moon-equipment`, 2026-10-01): `prepare()` copies `zombie_black_hole_bomb`/`zombie_quantum_bomb` from `moon/combat-data.json` into `data.equipment` and adds them to `boxPool`; `setup()` loads their world models into `mysteryBox.models`. The box gives prizes with `session.giveWeapon`, which is wrapped to give these 3 at a time into a tactical slot shared with Monkey Bombs (`giveMonkeys` is wrapped to clear it), refilled by Max Ammo. X (a capture-phase keydown that stops the game's monkey throw) plays the Moon throw animation (`view.equip(def)` + `fireAnim`), launches the world model at 0.38 s (520 forward, 170 up, gravity 650, one bounce), and it goes off 1 s after settling. Gersh: a purple swirl sprite plus PointLight for 10 s; zombies within 650 path to the nearest walkable point, and within 220 they're dragged through the air into it (spinning, scaled down by distance) and killed at the centre (cause `gersh`). The player walking in (<70) is teleported to the inside spot of a random active barricade. QED: one of 11 outcomes (Max Ammo, quantum explosion, free perk, teleport, ±1000 points, Double Points, lose a perk, empty mag, 4 zombies, Pack-a-Punch the gun in hand). HUD `#tactical-hud`; `window.kino.tactical`.
- **Wonder weapons from the user's own install** (`mods/wonder-weapons`, 2026-10-01): BO1 is installed (Steam, base game only: Kino, Five, the CoD5 remakes are patch-only; the DLC zones for Der Riese/Call of the Dead/Shangri-La are not installed) at `~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/Call of Duty Black Ops`. OpenAssetTools is built from source at `~/.local/opt/OpenAssetTools` (x64 release, `build/bin/Release_x64/Unlinker`; build with `./generate.sh` then `make -C build config=release_x64 UnlinkerCli`, ~11 min). Dumps go to `export_game/<zone>` (git-ignored). `.tools/build_wonder_weapons.py "<game dir>"` turns the dumps into `export/web/mods/wonder-weapons/{weapons.json,models,textures,animations,audio}`; these are git-ignored (built from the user's copy, not redistributed), so a fresh checkout needs a rebuild. Textures are converted with ffmpeg (no Pillow on the host, and no python venv without sudo). Resident sounds come from `common_zombie.ff` via `extract_resident_audio.py`'s `records`/`container`.
- **Winter's Howl** (`freezegun_zm`, PaP Winter's Fury), in the box pool and classed as a Wonder weapon. Tuning comes from `_zombiemode_weap_freezegun.gsc`: a cylinder of radius 120 along the view out to 600, damage 500→1000 by distance (PaP 180/900, 750→1500), excluding zombies behind the player or blocked by walls. Survivors are slowed (×0.6). Kills (cause `freeze`) play one of the 5 `ai_zombie_freeze_death_*` clips (`rig.add(key, data)` takes the JSON, not a clip) and ice over (cloned light-blue materials). The statues shatter when hit by another freeze blast or a bullet ray (radius 180, 500→250 to nearby zombies; PaP 300, 750→500), crumble when walked into (<34) or after 12 s. Sounds: front + rear shot layers, the reload notetracks (`sndnt#fly_freeze_*` intercepted in `view.onSound`), freeze/impact/shatter/collapse. Freeze kills earn weapon XP and camo progress.


## Death Machine wind-up, reload and upgrade (2026-10-01)

In `moon-weapons`: the barrels must spin up (0.75 s, 0.45 s Pack-a-Punched) before `session.fire` lets a shot through; holding aim (right mouse) or the trigger spins them, with the Moon start/loop/stop sounds and the `j_barrel` bone rotating. Reload is 3 s: the gun drops off screen (pivot offset) with LMG belt/latch sounds. It was never Pack-a-Punchable in BO1/BO2 (a power-up there), so the upgrade is our own: "The Reaper", 750/1500, 900 damage.

## Pack-a-Punch camo (2026-10-01)

`camo.js` `PAP_CAMO`: BO1's `camo_packapunch_c` base with `camo_packapunch_env` as a circuit mask whose traces glow in a purple-to-blue wave (`camoTime`, advanced by weapon-levels). Upgraded guns that reuse the stock model wear it in place of their chosen camo; upgrades with their own model (Ray Gun, wonder weapons) don't. Mipmapping averages the thin traces toward grey, hence the low `smoothstep(.36,.62)` threshold.

## Gersh light

The Gersh black hole used to add a `PointLight` to the scene: adding or removing a light recompiles every lit shader (a multi-second freeze each time). It now pushes a `dynamic:true` source into `kino.lighting.sources`, which the fixed light pool picks up.

## Black Ops III assets (Greyhound) and the Wunderwaffe DG-2

BO3 (`/mass_drive/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Call of Duty Black Ops III`) can't be read by OpenAssetTools; its assets come from the running game through Greyhound 1.46.3.2, copied to `C:\greyhound` in the BO3 Proton prefix (`compatdata/311210`).

**Exporting**
- Greyhound must run **inside the game's Steam Runtime container**, or it can't see the game (each container has its own wineserver). Set the BO3 launch options to `STEAM_COMPAT_LAUNCHER_SERVICE=proton %command%`, load a map, then `~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/SteamLinuxRuntime_4/pressure-vessel/bin/steam-runtime-launch-client --bus-name=com.steampowered.App311210 --directory='' -- wine 'C:\greyhound\Greyhound.exe'`. Protontricks starts a separate container: no good. The prefix needed `vcrun2022` (mfc140u.dll).
- `greyhound.json`: `exportmodelimg` off (exporting a model *with* its images froze Greyhound), `cdn_downloader` off, `showximage` on. Export images separately by searching their names (`i_wpn_t7_zmb_dg2`).
- Driving it: BO3's window can't be screen-captured (Vulkan), Greyhound's can (`ffmpeg -f x11grab -window_id`). xdotool clicks work on a fresh Greyhound, but **after one export its input stops**: kill it and relaunch for each batch. Its list never shows a selection, so search the exact name and use Export All (it exports what the list shows).
- Exports are copied to `export_game/bo3/` (git-ignored): `xmodels/`, `xanims/` (SEAnim), `ximages/` (PNG), `sounds/`.
- **Sounds** don't need the game: Greyhound's Load File opens BO3's sound banks (`zone/snd/all/*.sabl`, 680 of them; type the relative path, e.g. `zone\snd\all\zm_common.all.sabl`, into the dialog's File name). Names have no folders, so Export All the bank (`keepsndpath` keeps `wpn/energy/dg2/...`) and copy the weapon's folder. Find which bank has a weapon with `strings -n 8 <bank>.sabl | grep -i <name>` (the DG-2: `zm_common`; The Giant's `zm_factory` has a patched fire). The build converts them to Ogg (`audio/`, keyed by path in weapons.json `sounds`).

**Building** (`python3 .tools/build_bo3_weapons.py`): `.tools/semodel_to_glb.py` (SEModel → skinned GLB, Z-up → Y-up, CoD winding flipped, WebP textures via EXT_texture_webp, roughness from BO3 gloss), `.tools/seanim.py` (SEAnim reader) → `mods/bo3-weapons/` (models/, animations/ in the xanim_to_json clip format, weapons.json; all git-ignored).
- BO3 first-person rigs match BO1's layout (tag_view > tag_ads > tag_torso > shoulders) at about **2.2× the scale**; that's harmless, the camera sits at tag_view. Each BO3 weapon carries its own BO3 arms as `handsModel` (Dempsey's viewhands).
- Renames: `tag_weapon_right` → `tag_weapon` (the mount ViewWeapon attaches to); the gun's own root `tag_weapon` track is dropped (it's the anchor). BO3 finger joints `_1.._3` are BO1's `_0.._2` (BO3's `*base` joints are extra helpers): shifted so `fp-arms.js` finds them.
- Gun-part translations: BO3 already stores them as offsets from the part's bind (bulbs and bolt are 0 at rest; the bulb clip parks itself ~90 behind the gun out of view and is brought in during the reload), which is what ViewWeapon expects (it adds the bind back), so they're written as is. Subtracting the bind (the first build) put the bulbs and the bolt (with the release handle on it) at the gun's origin: they vanished on reload and fire. Hands translations are absolute.
- `fp-arms.js` now builds the character's first-person arms **per hands rig** (keyed by the hands geometry): built once from BO1's hands they were placed wrong on BO3's rig.

**Wunderwaffe DG-2** (`tesla_gun_zm`, Mystery Box; PaP: Wunderwaffe DG-3 JZ): `mods/bo3-weapons/mod.js`. A shot arcs to the zombie nearest the aim (out to 1000) and chains from each kill to the next within 300, up to 10 kills (PaP 1200 / 420 / 24), one kill every 0.12 s, with jagged additive arcs. Sounds are BO3's own: the layered player shot (front/LFE/rear; a separate last shot), flux + impact per arc, reload foley on the reload animation's `sndnt#wpn_tesla_*` notetracks (via `view.onSound`), and the idle hum while it's in hand. Clip 3, max ammo 12 (PaP 6 / 24). Verified headless (Sandbox): one shot, 5 of 6 zombies; fire/reload/raise animations play; Bondrewd's arms follow the BO3 rig.
- **Hip position**: weapons can set `viewOffset` [x, y, z] in view space (animation.js adds it to the viewmodel pivot, scaled by 1 − ADS so aiming still centres). The DG-2 uses [6, −8, −1]: low in the bottom-right corner like BO3.
- **DG-2 lights** (`mods/bo3-weapons/mod.js`, `updateLights`): the view gun's bulb/glow/power materials are cloned (materials are shared between model copies) and given BO3's glass emissive map (`textures/dg2_glow_e.webp`, from `i_wpn_t7_zmb_dg2_glass_e`, exported by the build). Bulbs lit = ceil(mag / clip × 3) (one goes dark per shot; two shots each on the DG-3 JZ), flicker, flare on firing; the glow tubes pulse; the power cell shows the charge. Electricity: jagged additive LineSegments plus soft glowing Points (WebGL lines are 1 px) parented to `tag_flash` (the prongs) and to each lit bulb's tag, regenerated every ~0.08 s; pink on the upgraded gun.

Mod sounds: Greyhound Load File on the mod's `snd/all/core_mod.all.sabs` (reload foley, names like `aug_clipin`, `weap_scar_saprtesla_*`) and `.sabl` (fire `aug-1..3`, `weap_scar_saprtesla_fire_plr`/`_final_plr`), exported with paths under `codolbf/weapons/<gun>/`; the build maps each animation's `sndnt#<name>` to `<name>.wav` (or `<name>_r`) and writes `sounds.mod` (shots, last, notes) + `sounds.audio` (key → ogg) to weapons.json. ## Weapon inspect (2026-10-03, `mods/inspect/`)

- **Input**: hold reload (R; X on a controller) for 0.35 s. `engine.js` times the hold (`reloadHeld`) and emits `inspect` to mods; a tap reloads on release, or at once when the mag is empty. The controller mod now holds R for as long as X is held (it used to tap it).
- **Clip inspect**: guns with `animations.inspectAnim` play it in the view's `inspect` mode, which `animation.js` leaves alone. `build_bo3_weapons.py` adds it from `<prefix>_inspect_full` or `<prefix>_inspect` (all five mod guns; the DG-2 has none), plus `inspectTime`. Some inspect foley notes have no exported sound yet (silent).
- **Procedural inspect** (BO1 guns, DG-2): after `view.update` the mod rotates `view.pivot` about `j_gun` through pose keys in `mod.js` (`KEYS`: turn the left side toward you, then roll to the right), 2.8 s.
- **Cancels** on fire (shot count), reload, melee, perk drink, weapon switch (`view.currentId`), aim or sprint. `engine.js` sets `view.input={sprint,ads}` each frame for this.
- **Glow cards**: BO3 effect-only glow materials (colour map `fullalpha_col`) used to render as solid boxes. `semodel_to_glb.py` now gives them a generated radial spot (blended, emissive in the gun's `glow` colour, passed by the build), and drops `mtl_hud*` in-world HUD quads and materials with no images at all (the sight attachments' reticle quads, drawn by BO3's reticle shader; they rendered white).
- **Cache busting**: heavy assets (glb/ogg/webp) are browser-cached for a day by `kino-server.mjs`, so `build_bo3_weapons.py` stamps model and texture URLs with `?v=<md5>`; a rebuild reaches players without a hard refresh.
- **Glow look** (`mods/bo3-weapons/glow.js` `applyGlow`/`tickGlow`, built once per model object; shared by the game, the Gunsmith view, the loadout thumbnails and the Operator first-person preview, which pass `depthTest:true` for halos where the gun can be turned around; the Gunsmith applies it before recording `emissive0` so its slot pulse doesn't wipe the glow): the converter tags BO3 glow materials with `extras.bo3glow` (glTF extras → `material.userData`). Light cards (`{card:true}`): additive, hot white core fading through the gun's colour, plus a halo sprite per cluster of card vertices (clustered within 3 units, skinned positions), parented to the vertex's bone so it follows animation (viewmodels get no bloom). Animated glows (`{scroll,tint}`): BO3's real glow image, whose pattern lives in its **alpha** (the RGB is often plain white), embedded as RGBA PNG ≤256 px (the webp path drops alpha) and scrolled in u at runtime. The mapping and speeds are guesses in `build_bo3_weapons.py` `GLOW_MAPS` (`_ani` = animated, `lowspeed` = slow; BO3's scroll parameters aren't exported). Each light flickers on its own phase; all flare on a shot. Sight lenses (`_ret`/`lens`, not glow) are a faint additive tint (they rendered as lit panels). P90 Orbit glow colour is orange to match its orbit texture.
