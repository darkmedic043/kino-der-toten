# Weapons: Moon, Thundergun, animation fix, ammo bulbs

- **Moon wonder weapons mod** (`mods/moon-weapons`): `prepare()` copies `microwavegundw_zm`, `microwavegun_zm` and `minigun_zm` from `moon/combat-data.json` (asset paths are already web-root relative) with projectileSpeed and explosionRadius set to 0 (hit-scan), and adds the Wave Gun and Death Machine to `boxPool`. `setup()` merges the Moon `wpn/microwave` and `lmg/minigun` audio cues, wraps `audio.weapon`, makes Zap Gun bullet hits lethal via `beforeEnemyDamage`, runs the Wave Gun cone kill (range 1000/1400, dot > 0.78) when `session.shots` increments, and B swaps dw ↔ combined (separate ammo stored on `weapon.other`; the first swap gives the other form the same share of its ammo, and a wrapped `session.powerup('full_ammo')` refills the stashed form too).
- **Thundergun mod** (`mods/thundergun`): the exported Thundergun glTFs lost their alpha modes, so the mod patches the shared cached materials once (loaded via `loadModel`, so the viewmodel, box display and third-person hold are all fixed): alphaTest 0.5 only on the wire mesh (the body texture's alpha is not a cut-out mask: cutting it removed the dashed ammo ring and ribbed strips); `chamber_glass` and the lens become transparent; `chamber_main` becomes an additive, emissive, scrolling energy texture that flares on each shot. It wraps `audio.weapon` ('shot' + base id `thundergun_zm`) to spawn a shockwave in the world scene (streaked additive cone, three expanding rings, muzzle flash sprite, dust points, and a screen flash overlay) sized to the game's 600-unit, dot > 0.65 kill cone. It tags `cause==='thunder'` victims in `beforeEnemyDamage` and throws their corpses on `kill` (ballistic arc, wall and ground raycasts, backward tilt); skipped for `host.remoteDamage`.
- **Weapon-part animation fix** (upstream `animation.js` edit): T5 viewmodel xanim *translations* on weapon-model bones (bolts, magazines, pumps, straps, the Thundergun's `tag_pump` → rig → `tag_clip` drum, the Zap Gun/Wave Gun parts) are displacements from the bone's bind position, while rotations are absolute. `makeClip` used to apply them as absolute, which pulled parts into the receiver: the Thundergun's drum (its magazine) vanished into the body and its barrel floated detached, the reload barely moved, and the MP40 magazine sat about 4 units off. `ViewWeapon.equip` now stores `userData.bindPosition` on every gun and left-gun bone except `j_gun`/`j_gun1` (anchors, or animated perk bottles), and `makeClip` adds it to position keys. Hands and locomotion clips are unchanged. Evidence: 1,848 gun position tracks look like pure single-axis offsets (e.g. M1911 empty slide −2.16,0,0; Ithaca pump 4.06,0,0); rotation tracks mostly match the bind pose (absolute).
- **Thundergun ammo bulbs** (`mods/ammo-counters`): the original showed rounds in the magazine as red lights on the drum (FX on `tag_bulb1-8`, not exported). The mod wraps `view.equip`, adds an additive red sprite to each `tag_bulb` bone of the Thundergun viewmodel, pushes each sprite 0.9 out along the gun’s −X (the tags sit 0.15 behind the drum face, so the glow was buried), and lights bulb n while `mag ≥ n` (2 normally; Pack-a-Punch 4, but the model has no `tag_bulb4`, so 3 show, as in the original). The Zap Gun's "87" is a printed decal in the original, not a counter.
- **Zap Gun ↔ Wave Gun transitions** (moon-weapons mod): the Zap Gun rig's `altDropAnim` (dw_2_combo, 1.73 s, with `wpn_micro_rld_join`) plays before swapping to the Wave Gun; splitting equips the Zap Guns and plays `altRaiseAnim` (combo_2_dw, 1.33 s, with `wpn_micro_rld_separate`). `equipInPlace` skips the normal raise, firing is locked via `session.fireLeft`, and the other form's model and animations are preloaded while either is held.
- **Thundergun reload sounds** (thundergun mod): `notetrackSounds` maps `sndnt#fly_thundergun_cell_lock` to `fly_thundergun_cell_slide_lock`, and the mod plays `fly_thundergun_eject` at 6% and the charging beep `fly_thundergun_cell_on` at 76% of the reload (both have no animation cue).
- **Gersh Device and QED** (`mods/moon-equipment`, 2026-10-01): `prepare()` copies `zombie_black_hole_bomb`/`zombie_quantum_bomb` from `moon/combat-data.json` into `data.equipment` and adds them to `boxPool`; `setup()` loads their world models into `mysteryBox.models`. ## Death Machine wind-up, reload and upgrade (2026-10-01)

In `moon-weapons`: the barrels must spin up (0.75 s, 0.45 s Pack-a-Punched) before `session.fire` lets a shot through; holding aim (right mouse) or the trigger spins them, with the Moon start/loop/stop sounds and the `j_barrel` bone rotating. Reload is 3 s: the gun drops off screen (pivot offset) with LMG belt/latch sounds. It was never Pack-a-Punchable in BO1/BO2 (a power-up there), so the upgrade is our own: "The Reaper", 750/1500, 900 damage.

## Pack-a-Punch camo (2026-10-01)

`camo.js` `PAP_CAMO`: BO1's `camo_packapunch_c` base with `camo_packapunch_env` as a circuit mask whose traces glow in a purple-to-blue wave (`camoTime`, advanced by weapon-levels). Upgraded guns that reuse the stock model wear it in place of their chosen camo; upgrades with their own model (Ray Gun, wonder weapons) don't. Mipmapping averages the thin traces toward grey, hence the low `smoothstep(.36,.62)` threshold.

## Gersh light

The Gersh black hole used to add a `PointLight` to the scene: adding or removing a light recompiles every lit shader (a multi-second freeze each time). It now pushes a `dynamic:true` source into `kino.lighting.sources`, which the fixed light pool picks up.

## Black Ops III assets (Greyhound) and the Wunderwaffe DG-2

BO3 (`/mass_drive/SteamLibrary/steamapps/common/Call of Duty Black Ops III`) can't be read by OpenAssetTools; its assets come from the running game through Greyhound 1.46.3.2, copied to `C:\greyhound` in the BO3 Proton prefix (`compatdata/311210`).

**Exporting**
- Greyhound must run **inside the game's Steam Runtime container**, or it can't see the game (each container has its own wineserver). Set the BO3 launch options to `STEAM_COMPAT_LAUNCHER_SERVICE=proton %command%`, load a map, then `~/.local/share/Steam/steamapps/common/SteamLinuxRuntime_4/pressure-vessel/bin/steam-runtime-launch-client --bus-name=com.steampowered.App311210 --directory='' -- wine 'C:\greyhound\Greyhound.exe'`. Protontricks starts a separate container: no good. The prefix needed `vcrun2022` (mfc140u.dll).
- `greyhound.json`: `exportmodelimg` off (exporting a model *with* its images froze Greyhound), `cdn_downloader` off, `showximage` on. Export images separately by searching their names (`i_wpn_t7_zmb_dg2`).
- Driving it: BO3's window can't be screen-captured (Vulkan), Greyhound's can (`ffmpeg -f x11grab -window_id`). xdotool clicks work on a fresh Greyhound, but **after one export its input stops**: kill it and relaunch for each batch. Its list never shows a selection, so search the exact name and use Export All (it exports what the list shows).
- Exports are copied to `export_game/bo3/` (git-ignored): `xmodels/`, `xanims/` (SEAnim), `ximages/` (PNG), `sounds/`.
- **Sounds** don't need the game: Greyhound's Load File opens BO3's sound banks (`zone/snd/all/*.sabl`, 680 of them; type the relative path, e.g. `zone\snd\all\zm_common.all.sabl`, into the dialog's File name). Names have no folders, so Export All the bank (`keepsndpath` keeps `wpn/energy/dg2/...`) and copy the weapon's folder. Find which bank has a weapon with `strings -n 8 <bank>.sabl | grep -i <name>` (the DG-2: `zm_common`; The Giant's `zm_factory` has a patched fire). The build converts them to Ogg (`audio/`, keyed by path in weapons.json `sounds`).

**Building** (`python3 .tools/build_bo3_weapons.py`): `.tools/semodel_to_glb.py` (SEModel → skinned GLB, Z-up → Y-up, CoD winding flipped, WebP textures via EXT_texture_webp, roughness from BO3 gloss), `.tools/seanim.py` (SEAnim reader) → `mods/bo3-weapons/` (models/, animations/ in the xanim_to_json clip format, weapons.json; all git-ignored).

## Black Ops 7 weapons, rebuilt (2026-10-09, `mods/bo7-weapons`)

User decision: BO7 guns are **rebuilt from reference**, not ripped (BO7 runs on Ricochet; Cordycep/Saluki carry ban and takedown risk). They are this fork's own assets, so they ship in Rind installs and can be public.

**Method** (proven on the Grimhawk): model the gun in Blender directly in a BO1 donor gun's `j_gun` space (CoD axes: x forward, y left, z up, inches), as objects `part_<bone>` (`part_j_gun`, `part_tag_clip` = the magazine, `part_j_charging_handle`). `.tools/assemble-grimhawk.mjs` copies the donor viewmodel's skeleton and skin (inverse bind matrices) untouched, drops its meshes and weights each part 100% to its bone, so every donor animation (reload with the mag on `tag_clip`, fire, sprint, ADS) drives the new gun; `tag_flash` is moved to the new muzzle. Blender's Z-up→Y-up export lands the vertices in the donor's bind space (x, z, −y). It also writes a static world model shifted into the donor world model's frame (M16: origin near the handguard, −10.5/−2.55). The weapon def is the donor's (animations, timings, sounds, upgrade) with the model, stats and `hideTags: []` replaced. Models sit at the mod root (Rind skips `models/` folders; an `OWN` rule includes `mods/bo7-weapons/*.glb`).
- **ADS**: the sight must sit on the donor's sight line.
