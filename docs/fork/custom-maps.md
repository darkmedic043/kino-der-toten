# Custom zombies maps

- **Custom zombies maps**: `custom-world.js` (the `CustomWorld` class) builds a world from a glTF plus named markers (`PLAYER_SPAWN, ZSPAWN, WINDOW, DOOR, WALLBUY, PERK, BOX, PAP, POWER, CLAYMORE, TRAP, TRAPZONE, TELEPORT, TPDEST, EGG`); glTF lights are scaled by scale² for the inch units and `POWERLIGHT_*` lights follow the power; dog rounds fade in fog; unusable markers go to `world.warnings`, which is shown on the map's start menu. It exposes the same interface as upstream `world.js`, so upstream `Enemies`, `MysteryBox` (fed stand-in `data.entities`), `Powerups`, `PerkDrink` and `KinoFeatures` run unchanged. The navmesh is generated in the browser with `vendor/recast-generators.mjs` (a copy of `@recast-navigation/generators` 0.43.1 in the importmap), tiled so door blocking (poly flags) stays local. `zombies.{html,js,css}` is a trimmed copy of `game.js`'s loop (Kino's teleporter, reels and Easter eggs are left out; generic traps and teleporters come from markers: `updateTraps` and `updateTeleporters` in zombies.js, `buildTraps` and `buildTeleporters` in custom-world.js). Machine models face local +Z and markers face local +X, hence `FACE=π/2`.
- `.tools/make-sandbox-map.py` generates the Sandbox example map (uses every marker). Map thumbnails are in `mods/maps/thumbs/`.
- **World at War collision (2026-10-19, waw-ports.md Malibu Drive)**: `NCOL_` meshes are navmesh-only (monster clip); `TRAVERSE_<n>` markers with `extras.to` become one-way off-mesh links (fence hops, climbs, jump-downs: a zombie hops the gap as at a narrow doorway); a DOOR's `extras.clip` [min, max] joins its collision box; `path()` allows 4096 polygons and the query 65535 nodes.
- **Clip and traversal hops (2026-10-19)**: `CLIP_` meshes collide with the player's capsule only (bullets, grenades, zombies pass: `world.clipCollision`); a TRAVERSE marker's `extras.both` makes its link two-way, link radius 60; `world.traversals` and `path()` mark a link's far end `hop` (enemies.js: straight there, no replanning midway).
- **Traversal clips and door headroom (2026-10-28, waw-ports.md Nuketown round 3)**: on maps with `zombieWalls`, a zombie on a hop link plays BO1's traversal clip (`hopClip`: far end over 30 lower = jump down by height, over 40 higher = climb, else a top over 10 = vault): it walks to where the clip starts (edge or top of the link minus the clip's own lead-in), plays it moving by the clip's forward share stretched to the link (rate .85 + speed/220, 1-1.8, x1.5 climbing; vaults stay level and are lifted only over tops above 40), then walks off. Window entries on every map play a vault (`z.enter`). Zombies don't start an attack mid-hop. `doorHeadroom` stops at the first surface above a door.
- **Door blocks follow the door (2026-10-28, waw-ports.md Nuketown back porches)**: with `doorMargin`/`doorHeadroom` set, a closed door's player block is an oriented box (`footprint()` in custom-world.js: the smallest rectangle around the door's pieces seen from above, 1° steps, square-on unless 3% smaller). Its thickness grows by at most 6; each end of its width grows only to the door frame (rays along the door at 20/35/50/65 above its bottom, 72 long, block stops 2 into the frame), or 6 when nothing is within reach (a door standing in the open). `extras.clip` adds its height always, its footprint only within 5° of square-on (around an angled door it is a box of angled brushes as wide as the porch). The headroom stops at the first surface above (three rays up). The part keeps `obb` {x, z, c, s, hu, hw, y0, y1, vu, vw} (`vu`/`vw`: the door's own half extents), `plug` is its AABB, and `doorEject` tests and exits in the door's frame. The navmesh cut still uses the visible AABB + 5.
- **Stranded spawns and headroom (2026-10-19)**: map entry `strandedSpawns: {keep: [zone groups]}` moves a ZSPAWN that can't walk to the start zone's to the nearest walkable point that can (within 1500); `navHeight` sets the zombie headroom (default 70).
- **Spiral stairs and headway (2026-10-19)**: World at War builder cfg `spiralStairs` chains two-way links up a spiral stair's wedge steps; enemies.js moves a chasing zombie that makes under 12 units of headway in 1.2 s to the first path point over 25 away (within 130).
