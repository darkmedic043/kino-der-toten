# Props

## Terminal booth (`mods/terminal-booth`)

A payphone-style wall booth whose "phone" is a CRT terminal with a retro keyboard (coiled cord), plus a handset on a hook, corrugated hoses, back-wall conduits with sagging cable runs, glass coolant tubes, a junction box and live wiring. The user wants it on several maps; Kino is the first.

- **Model**: generated entirely by `.tools/build-terminal-booth.py` in Blender 5.2.2. Blender is a portable install at `~/.local/opt/blender` (symlinked to `~/.local/bin/blender`, no sudo). Rebuild: `blender -b -P .tools/build-terminal-booth.py -- export/web/mods/terminal-booth/terminal_booth.glb [--nobake] [--render <dir>]` (about 14 min per variant with the bake on the GTX 1660 Ti via CUDA; `--render` writes Cycles preview PNGs). Units are inches. The back is at local z=0, and the booth faces local +Z. It is 44 wide, 30 deep with the keyboard ledge, and 98 tall (the conduits reach 132).
- **Terminal** (rebuilt 2026-09-30): an all-in-one retro terminal on the cabinet, modelled on PET/VT-series units, replacing the old CRT box, shelf and loose keyboard. The booth exterior was kept as the user asked. Housing profiles are extruded, then boolean-cut (EXACT, cutter materials transferred) and bevelled via modifiers, applied with `new_from_object` (`solid()`). Parts: a hooded monitor with the face tilted back, a chamfered rounded screen recess (dark `bezel` walls) and a bulged `crt_screen` 17.6×13; hood and side vent slots; a brass nameplate with raised "MelonTerm 2.7"; two knobs, a rocker and `led_power`. The sloped deck has a recessed keyboard well and speaker slots. The keyboard has sculpted, dished keycaps (row tilt profile, 0.9 pitch), a staggered layout with a function row, a numeric keypad and two missing caps, and legends as real text geometry in two unbaked meshes (`key_legends_dark/light`).
- **Texturing**: procedural Cycles materials (`grimy()`: edge chipping from bevel-normal vs normal, worn-through patches on flat faces, two sets of scratches, AO crevice dirt, floor-height dirt, rust streaks and rust patches, dust on upward faces, hazard stripes; made grimier on 2026-09-30 at the user's request) are baked per group (`GROUP` = `term`, `front`, `shell`; each joined separately) into their own set: base colour 2048², tangent normal 2048² (bevel-node rounded edges plus chip and scratch depth) and packed ORM 1024², at 128 samples. WebP q92 inside the GLB, about 8.6–9 MB each, stored in LFS. Wear model: crisp edge chips plus bigger bites, worn patches, four sets of sharp scratches concentrated in scrape zones, rust streaks and patches, crevice dirt and dust on top faces. The mod sets max anisotropy on the booth textures. Gotchas: the DIFFUSE colour bake returns black for metallic surfaces, so the metallic links are cut before that bake and restored after. Plastics use `bare_metal=False`, so worn edges don't turn metallic. `glTF Material Output` node group feeds occlusion.
- **Separate live meshes** (not baked, found by node name): `crt_screen` and `sign_glow` (quads with 0..1 UVs; use a canvas with `flipY=false`), `wire_*` (sweeps with u = arc length × 0.01), `led_*`, `coolant_*` (v along height), `glass_*`, `spark_*` empties at the severed wire ends.
- **Mod**: `placements.json` lists spots per map id (`kino` for Kino, otherwise `api.map.id`) as `{position, yaw, model?, scale?}`, plus the sign text. Each spot is snapped to the floor and flush to the nearest wall behind it (collision rays). It adds a 44×100×30 box to `world.dynamic` (`window:true`). The screen is a 512×384 canvas redrawn at 15 Hz: boot lines, then idle status lines; "NO MAINS POWER / STANDBY" until `session.power`. The sign (`sign` in placements.json, now "MelonTerm 2.7") is a weathered canvas: chips per letter, scratches, grime blotches and rust drips (seeded from the text, so they're stable), plus a whole-sign stutter and one failing letter that buzzes on its own (redrawn only when it toggles). Live wires have a pulse texture scrolling along u, and the severed ones spark (additive points plus a flash plus a WebAudio crackle into `audio.master`). LEDs blink per kind, coolant scrolls, and an additive floor glow sits in front. `window.kino.terminalBooth.booths[i].print(line)` / `.setSign(text)` are hooks for the user's later use. It runs before `map-materials` in mods.json, so its shaders are compiled during loading.
- **Kino spot** (moved there at the user's request): the lobby wall under the west staircase, next to the arch passage (collision wall x −208 for z 850–910; the *visible* wall is at x −200, 8 in front, so `snap` also raycasts the rendered meshes and takes the nearer hit). The stair stringer is about 87 above the floor, so this spot uses `terminal_booth_compact.glb` (`--compact`: no conduit risers or cable spans above the roof, and the vent moved forward) at `scale: 0.9`. The first spot was the spawn room's west wall pilaster `[-388, 82, 1338]` yaw 90.
- **The Giant spot** (2026-10-08): the start area's lower courtyard, against the plain concrete wall under the mainframe platform's railing (`[-190,3,-560]` yaw −90, snapped to (−172,−2,−560); compact model, the railing is above). Found by scanning the start area (navmesh reachable from spawn with the doors closed) for flat walls (9 rays within 3 units, headroom, clear floor in front). `clearDebris: false` (new placement option): The Giant's uneven snow floor counted as debris and the clearance cut a hole in it (423 triangles).
- **Interior light**: a `led_strip` emissive bar under the roof plus a real `PointLight` (#f2f6ff, `lightIntensity` 700 in placements.json, range 78×scale, decay 2, no shadow). It's added during loading and never toggled; only its intensity jitters.
- **Zombie pathing**: the navmesh is baked without the booth, and disabling polys (the door mechanism in `setDoors`) is too coarse for big lobby polys. So the mod wraps `world.path` (path segments crossing the footprint, which is 22·s+16 wide each side and 30·s+16 deep, get detours at its front corners) and `world.closest` (snaps inside the footprint are pushed out). Checked: a path along the wall from z 820 to 945 bends out to the corners at x −161.
- **Debris clearance**: loose planks in front of the Kino spot made standing at the terminal bumpy (the feet rode 80→83 on the plank edges). `clearDebris` removes the triangles that sit at most 10 above the floor and rise more than 0.8 above it, inside the booth's front area (±(22·s+34) wide, 30·s+60 deep): in rendered map meshes they're made degenerate (unshared geometry only), and in collision they're pointed at the lowest vertex of the map, then `bvh.refit()`. Kino: 38 render and 194 collision triangles.
- **MelonTerm desktop** (2026-10-08, `desktop.js`): F at the screen (within 80 of `b.screenAt`, the `crt_screen` bounds centre, in front of the booth and looking at it) opens a green-phosphor CRT window (920×580 max) floating over the game, which **keeps running** (user decision, 2026-10-08): the engine's `pointerlockchange` handler ignores losing the lock while `mods.keepActive` is set (the mod sets it on the host), so the HUD stays up, zombies keep coming and drinks finish behind the window. desktop.js swallows key presses (not releases, so a key held when it opened doesn't stick) and clicks; mousemove only looks while locked. Going down or game over logs off. On close the pointer is recaptured (the Esc/click is the gesture); if that fails the game pauses so the resume button can capture it. The shop redraws every 0.25 s when owned/power/points change. Square app icons, a status bar (MAINS OK / AUX BATTERY, points, clock) and a taskbar (LOG OFF; Esc closes the app window, then logs off). The booth screen prints USER LOGGED IN / RUN PERKS.EXE / USER LOGGED OFF. Font: VT323 from Google Fonts with a Courier fallback.
- **Perks app = a perk shop for every perk in the game** (user decision 2026-10-08: perks are global, bought here on any map whether or not it has the machine). `PERKS` in desktop.js: the eight perks (Cheese Cube's Double-Dew left out until the user decides what it does). Hover shows icon, price, effects and whether the map has a machine; click (or the BUY button, Enter) buys. Purchases go through the map's own path: `api.shop.buyPerk` (engine; now in the mod api with `api.perkInfo`), or `session.buyPerk` + `perkDrink` on Moon; a perk missing from the table is added first. Rules: perks other than Quick Revive need the power (greyed out with NO POWER until then), owned ones are marked, Quick Revive depletes after 3, no buying mid-drink. The price is the perk table's (so Cheese Cube's PhD is 1000 there). Icons: BO1's four from `textures/`, the other four from perks-plus's git-ignored `icons/` (user-supplied BOCW HUD art: staminup, deadshot, mulekick, phd; letter badge if missing). The F prompt goes through the engine hook `mods.prompts` (`[{find(camera)->{text,distance}, use()}]`, checked in `findPrompt`, used in `interact`), so it works on every map module. Tested on Kino: Juggernog refused without power, Quick Revive bought for 500, Mule Kick (no machine on Kino) bought with power, drinks play, HUD icons show.
- **STARK app** (2026-10-10): the Iron Man armors (gameplay.md, STARK.EXE).
- **ARSENAL app** (2026-10-09): ammo mods for the gun in hand (gameplay.md, Ammo Mods); same tile/detail layout as PERKS.EXE.
- **EXFIL app** (2026-10-08): an icon in the desk's bottom-right corner (windows are inset 128 on the right to leave it visible), greyed out unless an exfil window is open (pulses when it is). EXFIL.EXE shows the status from `kino.exfil.status()` (zone, hold time, reward, windows, or the reason and the next window with its reward) and CALL EXFIL, which starts it and logs off. See gameplay.md (Exfil).
- Icons: all eight perks now use the user's BOCW/BO6 HUD art in perks-plus's git-ignored `icons/` (the original four fall back to BO1's `textures/` icons if missing); perks-plus also swaps them into the HUD.
- **Not done**: more desktop apps.
- Note: Animations run on the `update` event, which only fires while a game is live; headless shots need `kino.debug.resume(); kino.debug.step(n)`.
- **Beer tap** (2026-10-03, the user's plan: a Wunderfizz on it later, selling perks on maps that don't have them): a cyberpunk tap tower bolted to the outside of the left wing (local x −21 out to −31, y −20..−11, z 27..72): black chrome (`M['chrome']`) tower and crown, a spout over a grated drip tray with a pint (glass + beer + foam), a tall handle with a neon core, pink/cyan neon trim, a glowing beer line in a glass tube fed by a hose from the wing, a status LED, and `tap_screen` (a quad with 0..1 UVs on the tower's outer face). Names follow the booth's live-part rules: `led_tapstrip_*`/`led_tap_handle` (mode `neon`: steady with an occasional stutter), `glass_tap*`, `coolant_tap` (scrolling flow; keeps its amber instead of the coolant cyan). The mod draws vertical neon text on `tap_screen` ("ON TAP"; `booths[i].tapScreen.set(lines)` replaces it) and the collision box is 53 wide, shifted 4.5 left, to cover the tap. The preview has a `tap` camera.
- **Sandbox spot** (2026-10-15): the lobby's dividing wall, south half (`[-20,0,197]` yaw −90, snaps to x −6), facing the player spawn; the box and the trap switch are on the north half.
