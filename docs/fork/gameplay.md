# Gameplay mods: health, HUD, sounds, music, cheats

- **Music**: the `settings.music` toggle (default off); game-menu.js wraps `audio.playMusic` so 'ambience' only plays when it's on (Easter-egg songs unaffected).
- **Player health mod** (`mods/player-health`): `rules.player_health` 150 and `player_health_juggernog` 250 in its data.json. It overrides the `session.maxHealth` getter and refills on `reset` and after a Quick Revive (the game hardcodes 100). A zombie hit does 50, so 3 hits; dogs 40, novas 45.
- **Combat feedback mod** (`mods/hud`): synthesized WebAudio thumps (a sine pitch-drop punch plus a low-passed noise knock) for hits (higher for headshots, a second deeper thud on kills) and a heavier two-layer thump when hurt, through their own gain-2.4 bus and a compressor into `audio.master` (the game has no flesh or pain cues). The directional damage indicator is `#damage-ring` arcs; the attacker is inferred as the nearest enemy with `state==='attack'&&attackDealt` within 140 (the game doesn't pass it), and the arc is rotated by atan2(right, forward) each frame for 1.6 s.
- **Zombie voices mod** (`mods/zombie-sounds`): reads `sounds.json` {growl, attack, death: [files]} next to its clips. It replaces `enemies.onSound('growl')`, plays attack on a state change to attack and death on `kill`, with distance falloff to 1400 and random pitch. `.tools/split-sounds.py <audio> <folder>` cuts a compilation at silences (ffmpeg silencedetect), loudness-normalises it to ogg, and writes clips.json and a first-guess sounds.json. Until clips exist it logs one 404 and keeps the synthesized growl. YouTube downloads via yt-dlp were denied by the permission classifier, so the user fetches the audio themselves.
- **Cheat console** (`mods/cheats`): ` (Backquote) opens, when Settings → Dev → Cheat console is on, a text console (it pauses the game, and on close recaptures the pointer). Commands: help, weapons [filter], give/spawn <weapon> (partial names; Tab completes), pap, points, god, ammo, perk <jug|speed|doubletap|quick|all>, round <n>, killall, powerup <type>, heal, clear. The first cheat command sets `window.kino.cheats.used` until the next `reset` (new game), and shows a CHEATS · NO XP badge. The progression mod's `cheating()` then blocks XP, kills, best round and the results line; game.js and zombies.js skip the `kino.best*` write. Cheats are refused in co-op rooms (`kino.coop.code`). The console input stops key propagation, and game-menu.js's rebind remap ignores keys typed in text fields.

## Equipment HUD, perk icons, held throws, dismemberment (2026-10-01)

- **Equipment HUD** (`mods/hud/equipment.js`): bottom-right cluster under the ammo, lethal | tactical | claymores side by side, as greyed icons with counts (key hint above). Icons are rendered once from each item's world model (an offscreen renderer, then disposed); the frag is drawn on a canvas when its model isn't built. Hides the game's `#grenades` text line and replaces moon-equipment's text HUD.
- **Perk icons**: `hud.css` swaps the letter badges for BO1's `textures/specialty_*_zombies.png` by the badge's `title` (Kino's four perks only).
- **Held throws** (`mods/throwables/`): G/X brings the item up; hold to keep it, release to throw (leaves the hand `RELEASE` s into the throw clip). The M67 cooks: 3.5 s fuse from the pin, less left when thrown, blows up in hand (260 damage) at the fuse. Poses: every BO1 equipment clip *ends* with the hand out of view in Kino's rig (the monkey/frag clips don't animate `tag_weapon` at all, and the M67's own arm clips convert with the arm behind the camera), so the hold frame is picked by sampling the clip for the frame with the item best placed in view (cached per rig). Frag and monkey ride on the QED rig (it has a pull-pin clip) with its mesh hidden and the item's model (SkeletonUtils clone: the monkey is skinned) on `j_gun`. moon-equipment exposes `tactical.launch(id)` and skips its own X handler when `window.kino.throwables` exists. Frag assets: `build_wonder_weapons.py` → `data.equipment.frag_grenade_zm` (needs the `common_zombie_assets` dump).
- **Dismemberment** (`mods/dismemberment/`, assets from `.tools/build_dismemberment.py`, git-ignored): BO1's honor-guard gib parts share the body's 73-bone skeleton, so a hit zombie's body is split into `upclean` + `lowclean` halves bound to its live bones (`SkinnedMesh.bind` with the body's bones by name); limb loss swaps a half (`rarmoff_1`, `larmoff_1`, `legsoff_1`, `behead`) and launches the `*spawn` piece. The gib models name Kino's own body materials, so they wear the zombie's materials; gore surfaces get a dark red. Legs off → crawler (`ai_zombie_crawl`/`crawl_sprint`, crawl attack and death; speed ×0.45–0.55). Choice: the last bullet's hit point (wrapped `enemies.rayHit`) in the zombie's frame — low hits take legs, side hits above the waist take an arm, headshot kills can behead; explosions/Thundergun tear randomly. Debug: `kino.dismemberment.cut(id,'rarm'|'larm'|'legs')`, `.behead(id)`, `.state(id)`.
- **Reload sounds** (`mods/reload-sounds/`): reload notetracks name BO1 aliases that often don't match the sound file (`wpn_mp5_mag_out` → `fly_mp5_mag_out`, L96 `bolt_up` → `bu`), and the Ithaca, Python, Commando and AK-74u have no resident reload audio. A wrapper on `audio.notifyKey` renames, then borrows the same action from a sibling gun (the Python uses the .357's). `kino.reloadSounds.key(note,weaponId)` shows the resolution.
- **Max Ammo** (`mods/max-ammo/`) also fills every carried gun's magazine and underbarrel.
- **Gun sounds** (`mods/gun-sounds/`, layers from `.tools/build_gun_sounds.py`, git-ignored): BO1 player shots are layered; Kino ships only `plr/shot`. Adds the LFE thump (throttled to one per 90 ms) and the ring-off tail once the trigger is released, real suppressed shots (M16/AUG/SPAS/WA2000 families; others stay the quieter shot), the M16 shot for the Commando (BO1's zombies files have none of its own) and the RPG shot for the LAW. Guns without their own layer use their family's (table in the mod). Loads before `weapon-levels`, whose suppressed wrapper patches `audio.play`; this mod plays the real silenced cue through the unpatched `play`.
- Dismemberment fixes: the `behead` model is BO1's neck-stump *attachment*, not a torso (swapping it in deleted the upper body). Crawlers are hit-tested against a box around their bones (the game's body box is standing height), with standing zombies still using the game's test.
- **Effects** (`mods/fx/`): `effect()` in game.js/zombies.js now emits `'effect'` ({position,color,count,handled}) before drawing its cubes (upstream edit). The fx mod claims effects by colour — orange with count ≥12 = explosion (scaled by count), the default red = blood hit, green = Ray Gun, tan with count ≤4 = wall dust — and draws them with pooled sprites from BO1's `textures/fxt_*`: fireball atlas, glow flash, embers/sparks, rising smoke and floor dust for explosions; the 16-frame blood-burst flipbook, blood-cloud mist and gush for hits. No lights (adding one recompiles every shader). `kino.fx.explosion([x,y,z],scale)`, `.blood(p,amount)`.
- **Dive sounds** (movement mod): launch = cloth + arm swing + gear rattle; landing = a BO1 body fall (wood/dirt, from the gun-sounds build), gear rattle and a synthesized low thump.
- **Double Tap 2.0** (`mods/double-tap/`): on top of the base fire-rate boost, bullet damage ×2 while you have the perk (bullets only).
- **fx: Thundergun, Gersh, QED** (2026-10-01): the fx mod's colour matching compares raw sRGB hex (`new THREE.Color(hex)` converts to linear, so nothing matched at first and real explosions fell back to cubes). Thundergun shots use BO1's `fxt_fx_thundergun_ring` (from `.tools/build_fx_textures.py`, git-ignored; the Ray Gun ring stands in) rolling out with a smoke cloud, plus ground dust where flung zombies land; Thundergun hits make no blood and no gibs (they're thrown whole). The Gersh is a purple vortex (BO1 swirl/spiral smoke, contracting rings, sparks spiralling in) with an implosion when it closes; the QED a blue ring burst. Blood is capped per moment so many simultaneous hits don't fill the screen.

## Fall damage (2026-10-08, `mods/fall-damage`, after movement in mods.json)
- BO1's `bg_fallDamageMinHeight` 128 / `bg_fallDamageMaxHeight` 300: drops over 128 deal maxHealth·(h−128)/172, 300+ takes everything (solo without Quick Revive: game over). h is from the top of the arc (jumps and dive hops count) to the landing, times `player.gravity / refG` (refG = the strongest gravity seen since the first frame), so Moon's low gravity hurts by impact, not height (Moon sets gravity per frame in `prePlayer`; the movement mod's 1400 setup value is never seen there).
- Emits host `fall` {height, amount, dive, position} before `api.damage(amount)`; mods may lower `amount` (PhD). `dive` comes from the movement mod's `dive` event (BO1's `self.divetoprone`).
- Skipped: one-frame jumps over 150 (teleports, resets; a teleport into the air still counts the fall from where it appears), Moon jump pad flights (`api.features.flight`), reviving/gameover.
- Tested (Kino): jump and 120 fall 0; 170 → 38 of 150; 330 → down; Moon spawn 170 → 36.

## Exfil (2026-10-08, `mods/exfil`, after classes in mods.json)
- Cold War style. Windows: the start of round 11, 16, 21 … (`exfilRound`: r ≥ 11 and (r − 11) % 5 = 0), open for that whole round (preparing + fighting), so it closes when the round ends. Called from the terminal booth's EXFIL app (`kino.exfil.start()`); a toast says when a window opens (only on maps with a booth).
- Run: the landing zone is the farthest `zones.json` entry the player can reach (`world.path` ends within 90 of it, which respects closed doors; at least 240 away when possible; snapped with `world.closest`), else the player spawn. `enemies.autoSpawn`/`autoRounds` are switched off (restored after); a horde rises (`enemies.riseAt`, zombie-rise clips) at the 6 nearest spawn points more than 220 away, one every 0.55 s up to 28 alive, spawned with `session.round` temporarily +5 (their health and speed). 120 s; the last 25 s the chopper is landing. At 0, standing (not downed/dead) inside the zone (radius 160, ±120 high) = success, else left behind (game over, menu says "Left behind"; `finish(false)`).
- Marker: additive one-sided beam (one-sided, or standing inside it whites out the view) + floor ring and fill, pulsing faster while landing. HUD `#exfil-hud` top centre: timer, objective, distance in m.
- Success: game over as a win (`phase gameover`, `gameOver` emitted like Cheese Cube's ending, menu "Exfil successful"), reward with R = round − 1 rounds survived: account XP 20·R² (`kino.progression.grant`) and class XP 10·R² (`kino.classes.grantXp`, new; was 5·R², doubled 2026-10-09 at the user's request) — R 10: 2000/1000, R 15: 4500/2250, R 20: 8000/4000. Cheats: no bonus. Events `exfilStart`, `exfilEnd` {success, rounds, reward}.
- Kino zones: Theater, Alleyway, Dining Hall, Crematorium, Lobby (the Lobby is the only one before any door is opened). The Giant zones (2026-10-08, from a scan for navmesh spots reachable from spawn with ≥150 clear all round): Mainframe, Courtyard, East Yard, West Yard, Power Room, South/West/East Teleporter; from the terminal it picks the Courtyard with the doors shut and the South Teleporter with all of them open. (Test gotcha: teleporting the player high for overhead shots now kills them through fall damage; set `effects.invulnerable`.) Tested on Kino: window closed at round 1 / open at 11 and 16, horde of round-16 zombies (1846 health, speed 167), success paid 2000 + 500 and levelled both, failure outside the zone ended the game.
- **Co-op** (same day): host-authoritative like the rest of co-op. Anyone can call it: a guest's `start()` sends `{t:'exfil',a:'call',at}` to the host, which picks the zone from the caller's position (or answers `deny`). The host runs the timer and the horde (scaled: max alive 28 + 6 per extra player, spawn interval ÷ √players, spawned near a random standing player; the zombies reach guests through the normal snap mirror) and sends `{a:'state', zone, time, rounds}` every second — the field is `time`, because `t` is the relay's message type (a `t` timer overwrote it and guests dropped every message). Guests build the marker/HUD from the first state and follow its clock. At 0 the host judges every player (own feet + `net.remotes` poses, standing = not down/dead) and sends `{a:'end', escaped:[ids], rounds}`; each player wins with their own XP or is left behind. A guest that sees a game over first keeps `verdict` so the late `end` still applies. Handlers are added on the host `ready` event (the multiplayer mod loads after this one; a paused guest must still hear the host). A new host carries on from the last state (`takeOver()` on its next frame). HUD shows `SQUAD n/m IN`. Tested with two browsers: guest called it, both ran the same zone/timer with mirrored zombies; host in the zone + guest 450 away → host won (+4500/+1125), guest left behind; both in → both won with their own XP.

## Ammo Mods (2026-10-09, `mods/ammo-mods`, after exfil in mods.json)
- Cold War / BO6 / BO7 style: one mod per weapon (`session.weapon.ammoMod {id, tier}` on the weapon object, so it survives Pack-a-Punch, which keeps the object, and leaves with the gun), tiers I–III. Bought for the gun in hand at a terminal's **ARSENAL** app (under PERKS; laid out like PERKS.EXE — a growing grid of square icon tiles on the left, the hovered mod's tiers/price/button on the right; click applies or upgrades), only with the power on: apply 2500, II 4000, III 6000; swapping costs 2500 and restarts at I. `MODS` (id, name, colour, desc, `tiers` text) is the list the app shows; add entries there (an unknown id gets a letter icon; `MOD_GLYPH` in desktop.js holds the SVG glyphs). HUD: `#ammo-mod-tag` under the weapon name.
- Procs: player `bullet`/`projectile` hits (not remote, not melee) roll 7/10/14%; then the gun waits (`COOLDOWN`: 1 s, Dead Wire/Shatter 1.5 s, Brain Rot 6 s). Damage is a share of the zombie's max health (Shatter: of the round's zombie health), cause `ammo_mod`.
- Effects: Cryo (slow 40/55% for 4/5 s, II +15% damage taken, III frozen 3 s +25% then slowed; `z.speed` from a stored base and `rig.mixer.timeScale`), Napalm (30/45/60% over 3 s in 0.25 s ticks with `kino.fx.burn`, III spreads to a zombie within 120 each second), Dead Wire (chains to 2/4/6 within 250 in line of sight, 20/25/30% and a 1.2/1.6/2 s stun, jagged additive bolts), Shatter (radius 110/150/190, 60/80/100% of a round's zombie health, `kino.fx.explosion` tinted), Brain Rot (zombies only: turned for 6/9/12 s, goes for the nearest zombie and bites it for 25% of its max health a second, ignores the player; II: zombies within 300 go for it; III: explodes when it ends; dogs/specials get a small blast instead). Stuns and Brain Rot go through a wrapped `enemies.lureTarget` (a lured zombie never attacks the player; lured to its own spot = stands still). A coloured additive sprite marks the zombie for the effect's duration.
- Co-op: only the host simulates zombies, so slows/stuns/Brain Rot only work in a host's or solo game; a guest's procs still deal damage.
- Found on the way: the fx mod assigned `window.kino.fx` twice and the second wiped the first, so the tinted explosion exposed for PhD Flopper never reached it (it fell back to the plain blast). It now merges, and its `explosion` takes a Vector3 or [x,y,z] plus a tint.
