# Gameplay mods: health, HUD, sounds, music, cheats

- **Music**: the `settings.music` toggle (default off); game-menu.js wraps `audio.playMusic` so 'ambience' only plays when it's on (Easter-egg songs unaffected).
- **Player health mod** (`mods/player-health`): `rules.player_health` 150 and `player_health_juggernog` 250 in its data.json. It overrides the `session.maxHealth` getter and refills on `reset` and after a Quick Revive (the game hardcodes 100). A zombie hit does 50, so 3 hits; dogs 40, novas 45.
- **Combat feedback mod** (`mods/hud`): synthesized WebAudio thumps (a sine pitch-drop punch plus a low-passed noise knock) for hits (higher for headshots, a second deeper thud on kills) and a heavier two-layer thump when hurt, through their own gain-2.4 bus and a compressor into `audio.master` (the game has no flesh or pain cues). The directional damage indicator is `#damage-ring` arcs; the attacker is inferred as the nearest enemy with `state==='attack'&&attackDealt` within 140 (the game doesn't pass it), and the arc is rotated by atan2(right, forward) each frame for 1.6 s.
- **Zombie voices mod** (`mods/zombie-sounds`): reads `sounds.json` {growl, attack, death: [files]} next to its clips. It replaces `enemies.onSound('growl')`, plays attack on a state change to attack and death on `kill`, with distance falloff to 1400 and random pitch. `.tools/split-sounds.py <audio> <folder>` cuts a compilation at silences (ffmpeg silencedetect), loudness-normalises it to ogg, and writes clips.json and a first-guess sounds.json. Until clips exist it logs one 404 and keeps the synthesized growl. YouTube downloads via yt-dlp were denied by the permission classifier, so the user fetches the audio themselves.
- **Cheat console** (`mods/cheats`): ` (Backquote) opens, when Settings → Dev → Cheat console is on, a text console (it pauses the game, and on close recaptures the pointer). Commands: help, weapons [filter], give/spawn <weapon> (partial names; Tab completes), pap, points, god, ammo, perk <jug|speed|doubletap|quick|all>, round <n>, killall, powerup <type>, heal, clear. The first cheat command sets `window.kino.cheats.used` until the next `reset` (new game), and shows a CHEATS · NO XP badge. The progression mod's `cheating()` then blocks XP, kills, best round and the results line; game.js and zombies.js skip the `kino.best*` write. Cheats are refused in co-op rooms (`kino.coop.code`). The console input stops key propagation, and game-menu.js's rebind remap ignores keys typed in text fields.

## Fall damage (2026-10-08, `mods/fall-damage`, after movement in mods.json)
- BO1's `bg_fallDamageMinHeight` 128 / `bg_fallDamageMaxHeight` 300 was too punishing (user, 2026-10-30), so MAX is 640 here: drops over 128 deal maxHealth·(h−128)/512, 640+ takes everything (solo without Quick Revive: game over). h is from the top of the arc (jumps and dive hops count) to the landing, times `player.gravity / refG` (refG = the strongest gravity seen since the first frame), so Moon's low gravity hurts by impact, not height (Moon sets gravity per frame in `prePlayer`; the movement mod's 1400 setup value is never seen there).
- Emits host `fall` {height, amount, dive, position} before `api.damage(amount)`; mods may lower `amount` (PhD). `dive` comes from the movement mod's `dive` event (BO1's `self.divetoprone`).
- Skipped: one-frame jumps over 150 (teleports, resets; a teleport into the air still counts the fall from where it appears), Moon jump pad flights (`api.features.flight`), reviving/gameover.
- Tested (Nuketown road, 2026-10-30): of 150, drop 130 → 2, 200 → 22, 300 → 52, 500 → 110, 640 → all. MIN stays 128 because PhD's dive flop fires on `fall` events. Nuketown zeroes the amount for any landing below y 1560 (the bunker shaft, the holes into hell).

## Exfil (2026-10-08, `mods/exfil`, after classes in mods.json)
- Cold War style. Windows: the start of round 11, 16, 21 … (`exfilRound`: r ≥ 11 and (r − 11) % 5 = 0), open for that whole round (preparing + fighting), so it closes when the round ends. Called from the terminal booth's EXFIL app (`kino.exfil.start()`); a toast says when a window opens (only on maps with a booth).
- Run: the landing zone is the farthest `zones.json` entry the player can reach (`world.path` ends within 90 of it, which respects closed doors; at least 240 away when possible; snapped with `world.closest`), else the player spawn. `enemies.autoSpawn`/`autoRounds` are switched off (restored after); a horde rises (`enemies.riseAt`, zombie-rise clips) at the 6 nearest spawn points more than 220 away, one every 0.55 s up to 28 alive, spawned with `session.round` temporarily +5 (their health and speed). 120 s; the last 25 s the chopper is landing. - **Pacing**:
  - Priority 3 always plays and cuts off the current line. Lower priorities wait while a line is playing.
  - Every line also waits for a global gap (`GAP`: 9 / 4.5 / 2.5 s for Rare / Normal / Chatty) and rolls its chance, scaled by `CHANCE`.
  - Each event deals from a shuffled bag, so no line repeats until all of them have played.
  - `kino.voice.say(ev, force)` plays a line from the console (`force` skips the checks).
- **Caption**: plain text with no blur or shadow, so it adds no new Skia shader mid-game. It is painted by `warmHud`. Settings → Voice subtitles turns it off.
- **Co-op**: your line goes out as `{t:'vox', v, f, n}` to the room. Teammates play it from your body (`kino.coopRemote(id).root`, fading out by 2200 units), with your operator's name on the caption. Their client loads that voice's lines.json on first use.
- **New voice**:
  1. Transcribe the recording. The method used: faster-whisper medium.en on CUDA, run on silence-split ranges, because its own VAD merged minutes of speech. The scripts are in `scratch/voice/`.
  2. Write `picks.json`, `{event: [{s, e, t}]}`.
  3. Run `python3 .tools/build-voice.py <audio> picks.json export/web/mods/operator-voice/voices/<id>`. It produces mono, loudness-normalised (-16 LUFS) Vorbis clips with 20/50 ms fades.

## Sound that cuts out: the audio watchdog (2026-11-22, `engine.js`, user: "audio completely cut off, can you fix this")

The report: in a solo Gorod Krovi game on Rind (Windows) all sound stopped and stayed off until a reload. The client log of that game
has no script error, no lost context and no failed promise, so nothing crashed; it shows a pause of about 16 s (the window lost focus)
about 40 s before the reload. That leaves three ways the whole game goes silent, all through the one master gain or its context
(`audio.js`: every sound → `audio.master` → destination), and the log could not tell which it was:

1. **The browser stopped the AudioContext.** Chrome suspends a context on its own after an output-device error (a headset switching
   off, Windows changing the default device). The engine resumed the context only on the next click on the pause menu.
2. **The engine left it suspended.** `suspendRendering` (tab hidden, `pagehide`) pauses the audio even where nothing else pauses
   (co-op's `keepRunning`), and `resumeRendering` never resumed it: a co-op player who looked away came back to a running, silent game.
   Reproduced headless on the live engine before the fix.
3. **The M key.** Upstream's hard-coded mute toggle sits next to the gobblegum keys (N, J), its only notice was a 2.5 s "Sound off"
   toast, and it also fired while typing in a text box.

What the engine does now (`audioGuard`, `audioCheck`, `audioPause`, `toggleMute`, after the `pagehide` listeners):

- **Watchdog.** On the context's `statechange` and `error` events, on `devicechange`, and once a second: if the game is running
  (`active`, or `mods.keepRunning()` in co-op, tab visible, match started) and the context is not `running`, it is resumed. A context
  that says `running` with a `currentTime` that has not moved for 3 s is restarted (suspend, then resume; at most 5 times a session).
  After 6 s without success a toast says the sound device is not answering. A paused solo game stays suspended as before.
- **Own pauses are stamped.** The engine pauses through `audioPause()` (not `audio.pause()`), which records the time: a `statechange`
  within 500 ms of it is the tail of our own pause and is resumed without a report.
- **On record.** Each incident sends `ev: "audio"` to the client log (`why`: `state`, `error`, `tick`, `stall`, `device`, then `back`
  with the ms it took; `mute` for the M key; at most 8 a session), so the next report can be read from
  `~/.local/share/kino/client-log.jsonl`.
- **M key.** Ignored in text boxes; while muted a small "SOUND OFF · PRESS M" badge stays at the top of the screen (plain text, no
  blur, so no new HUD shader), and the toast names the key. The volume setting is kept (game-menu.js wraps `audio.enabled`).

Rules: pause the game's audio only through `audioPause()`; a mod must not suspend `audio.ctx` while the game runs (the watchdog brings
it straight back). `kino.audioGuard` holds the counters (`fixes`, `kicks`, `sent`).

Test: `scratch/audio/test.mjs` (session script: sandbox map on the phone profile, `--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required`, the
client-log beacons routed away from the real log). Before: a context suspended behind the game's back stayed suspended, as did co-op
after hidden → visible. After: back in about 55 ms (statechange) or within a second (tick); a normal pause stays silent; pause and
play again at once reports nothing; a frozen clock is restarted once. Not reproduced on the user's Windows machine: the cause of that
one game is not known, the fix covers all three. Memory: the sandbox on the phone profile held 1.2 GB of renderer memory; the same
page on a desktop profile took 4.2 GB (don't drop `hasTouch`/`isMobile` from a sandbox test).
