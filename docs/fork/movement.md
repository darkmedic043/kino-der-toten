# Movement mod and stairs

- **Movement mod** (`mods/movement`): gravity 1400 (jumpSpeed recomputed for jumpHeight 39); groundAcceleration 1500, except the game's original 2600 on stairs (`state.stairs` is set for 0.35 s whenever `onFloor` flips without a jump); airAcceleration 320 only after a real jump (detected from the jump key) or 0.2 s airborne. It wraps `player.update(dt,input)`: stair snap-down (a ray ≤ stepHeight+4 after walking off an edge), a **one-button crouch** (C/Ctrl, tracked by its own key listeners in game time: tap toggles crouch, holding 0.35 s goes prone, a tap while sprinting at >220 slides; jump from crouch or prone stands; sprint cancels a toggled crouch; `input.crouch` is overridden). **Prone** swaps `crouchHeight` 30, `crouchEyeHeight` 17 and `crouchSpeed` 45 (stands only if `_canUseHeight`). **Slide** lasts 1.35 s with speed = 130 + (start−130)·(1−u)^1.7, start = min(490, h·1.6), applied after the controller step along a slowly steering dir; it ends early on a wall hit, emits a `slide` event, and plays the data.json `slideSound` file or a synthesized band-passed noise scrape, **mantle** (jump near a wall whose top is 19–74 above the feet with room to stand, checked by a ray pair plus `capsuleIntersect`: a scripted 0.28 s + rise/260 climb). Camera: head bob, landing dip only on real falls (>0.22 s airborne and vy<−260), strafe/slide/mantle roll, eased eye-height changes. The state is exposed as `kino.movement.state`. `applyStance()` in characters.js pitches the character root (prone +1.45 face-down, slide −0.85, mantle +0.35; YXZ order) and `setStance` turns the aim pose past the head when prone; co-op poses carry `k` (stance). The motionModel landing threshold is −300 (no crouch per stair).
- **Stairs** (upstream `player-controller.js` edit): a riser collision projected the walking speed away and `_tryStep` never restored it. Steps are now also tried when the last contact is a non-floor surface facing against the movement (dot < −0.3; an edge brushed while descending faces along it and is ignored), and a successful step restores the pre-collision horizontal velocity. Measured with a navmesh stair walker: Kino's real staircases climb 10–55% faster with 0–14 slow frames instead of up to 177; descents are unchanged.
- **Stair spring (2026-10-05, maps with `stairSmoothing`)**: the absorbed offset is eased by a critically damped spring (ω = 2 × `stairSmoothRate`) instead of the first-order ease, which restarted at every riser so the eye's climb speed pulsed about ±50% per step. Cheese Cube climb, real-time 60 fps: max camera second difference 2.13 → 0.55, RMS 0.195 → 0.109, eye speed steady at 1.3–1.55/frame. The eye trails the feet by about one step (12 units) while climbing and settles at the top.
- **Stair camera smoothing** (movement mod): grounded feet jumps of 1.5 to stepHeight+6 in one frame (step-ups and snap-downs) go into `stairOffset`, which the camera absorbs and eases out (rate 11/s, clamp ±24); bigger jumps reset it. Camera jerk on the big lobby-side staircase went from max 4.3 (21 frames > 1.5) to 1.5 (1 frame).
- **Slides on slopes** (movement mod): a slide starts if grounded or airborne < 0.25 s (slopes flicker), when sprinting above 170 (was 220; uphill is slower), and the start speed is at least 220 × 1.6. While sliding, the player is snapped to the ground below (reach stepHeight + speed·dt·1.5); the ground normal's horizontal part gives a downhill boost (gravity × 0.9, clamped −start to +420) and slows the slide timer downhill; the slide ends if stalled below 0.6 × endSpeed.
- **Debris smoothing** (2026-09-30): on flat ground (a down ray from the feet hits a surface with |n.y| > .985), grounded feet changes of 0.12–1.5 also go into `stairOffset`, so riding over planks and rubble doesn't jolt the camera. On ramps they pass straight through, so the camera doesn't lag the slope. Walking up to the terminal booth: max camera second difference 2.6 → 1.1.
- **Black Ops 7 movement** (2026-09-30; features per Dexerto/Destructoid: omnimovement, wall jumps, dive; tactical sprint is a perk in BO7 so it was left out):
  - Omnimovement: `game.js`/`zombies.js` sprint on Shift + any movement (not only W), and never while sliding or diving (so hip fire works mid-slide and BO1's lowered sprint pose doesn't play). Slides steer toward the input direction (`moveDir()`), not the camera.
  - Dive: holding crouch for 0.2 s while sprinting leaps along the move direction (speed max(380, 1.45 × current), 250 up), keeps its momentum in the air (air control would brake it) and lands prone. A tap still slides.
  - Wall jump (**off since 2026-10-05**, `WALL_JUMP=false`: the user found it firing when they meant to mantle): a fresh jump while airborne (>0.12 s) with a wall within radius + 20 (probed in 12 directions at feet +22 and +46) kicks off it: along-wall speed ×0.85, 290 out, 0.95 × jumpSpeed up. Max 3 per airtime, 0.28 s apart, not the same wall twice in a row; reset on landing. Events `dive`, `diveLand`, `walljump`.
  - Measured with key input on fresh pages: sideways sprint 285, slide 359 → 136, dive 362 airborne then prone by 0.5 s, wall kick 324 out and up.
- **First-person motion** (`mods/movement/fp.js`, added to the pivot after `view.update`): look inertia (springs on yaw/pitch deltas), strafe roll, takeoff sink and landing dip, slide cant, dive tuck, wall-jump kick; aiming damps most of it. Mantle: the gun pitches away around the camera (not translated, so the left shoulder stays within reach), and the left arm reaches for the ledge with two-bone IK. The target is `mt.hand` (ledge edge, 7 left) in camera space, clamped into view (z −12…−16, 7–19° down, 5–24° left), because the real ledge is usually below the frame and beyond the arm's ~22 reach. The wrist and fingers ease to the skeleton's bind pose (an open hand). The rig's animations don't key every arm bone, so IK edits are restored each frame (`restoreArm`/`sealArm`); otherwise they pile up. Third person and co-op have a `dive` stance (flat, pitch 1.25).
- **Sprint stair snags (2026-10-05, upstream `player-controller.js`)**: sprinting, the rounded capsule rides up a riser's edge and leaves the floor with a small upward speed; the step-up required `climbSpeedY<=0` and floor/coyote, so the next riser stopped the player (logged: every snag was a skipped step with vy 9–109 and coyote 0). Steps are now also tried when airborne under 0.35 s with vy ≤ 160 and no jump in the last 0.6 s (`_airTime`, `_jumpGuard`). Cheese Cube, 10 stair runs sprinting at 30 fps: speed dips below 45% went from 13 to 3, climbs ~15% faster; walking at 60 fps no dips (1 before).
- **Kino's Speed Cola ramp (2026-10-18, user: "stairs ... bump you down when you stand on them")**: the short flight down to the Speed Cola room (floor 236 -> 168, around (1490-1590, 95-207)) is a 30-degree ramp-like run of 6-unit risers in Kino's collision; standing still on it the capsule was pushed along the contact normal each frame and slid 20-92 units to the bottom (every other Kino stair held). `maps/kino.js` now sets `player: {slopeStick: true}` (as the BO3 maps do): walkable contacts push straight up. Verified headless at 60 fps (`kino.debug.step`) and at dt 0.1 (`player.update`): 0-2 units of drift, no bounce.
- **Camera feel guard (2026-10-17)**: the bob/roll/dip/stair state in the movement mod's update handler read the camera back every frame, so one non-finite value (a `camera.lookAt` with a bad argument in a test harness did it) stayed forever and took the player's velocity with it. The handler now reports the state once (`[movement] non-finite camera feel` in the console, with every input) and resets it.
- **Adaptive first-person motion (2026-10-19, user: "adaptive first person animations like bo7 ... super fluid, move with the player, giving it weight"; reference: three BO7 clips the user downloaded, read as frame strips)**: `mods/movement/fp.js` rewritten around damped springs whose stiffness and amplitude follow the weapon's weight (`weaponWeight(def)`: pistol .45, SMG .7, rifle 1, shotgun 1.15, sniper 1.35, LMG 1.55, launcher 1.4, wonder 1.2, dual-wield x.8; `def.weight` overrides; amplitude ~ weight^0.6, lag ~ weight^0.4). Layers, all added to the pivot after the engine's own update: look inertia and a bank into fast turns; movement inertia from the acceleration in the camera's frame (a sprint start pulls the gun back and down, a stop throws it forward, strafes swing and roll it; airborne it floats up while falling and sinks on the way up); a figure-8 bob paced by distance travelled (one cycle is two steps: stride 150 walking, 200 sprinting, 110 crouched, about 2.5-2.9 steps a second; the first version used 36-52 and shook at 11 Hz, which the user saw as jitter), which replaces the engine's on/off bob (`view.bobScale = 0`); breathing that quickens with exertion after sprinting; weight events (landing dip and nod by fall speed, crouch and stand dips, stair steps, a fire kick that overshoots and settles, an aim settle when the sights come up); a procedural BO7 sprint pose (next entry); the old slide, dive, wall-kick and mantle layers. Settings → Gameplay → Weapon motion (0-150%) scales it; a non-finite frame resets every spring. `kino.fpMotion.weight()`.
- **Framing** (same file, from the reference footage: the gun sits lower and further right than BO1's hip pose, and a touch further away): every BO1 rig gets a fixed shift (0.5, -0.7, -1.3 in view space); an imported BO3 rig (its own arms, about 2.2x the scale: told by the wrist spacing) is measured once at idle and its grip (`j_gun`/`tag_weapon`) moved to BO1's rifle grip position scaled, which replaces the corner offsets those guns had (the AK117's left hand was below the frame). Eased in over a quarter second, gone in ADS like `def.viewOffset`.
- **Hands on every gun**: an operator with its own model gets its arms re-skinned onto every rig (`fp-arms.js`); an operator without one (the Test Subject and its outfits: an arm set plus colours) used to show a BO3 weapon's own viewhands (`def.handsModel`, Dempsey's) merely tinted, one material for the whole arm, so the glove came out in the sleeve's colour. Since 2026-10-19 (user: "on some of the guns, it uses a different hand than what the operator being currently used is") the arm set itself is re-skinned onto imported rigs (`ArmSetArms`, see characters.md); the tint remains as the fallback.
- **Whole-view sway, sprint pose, adaptive hands (2026-10-19, later; user: "every gun animation should fit this new style, adaptive hand positioning ... swaying one way sways the entire fpv")**, all in `mods/movement/fp.js`:
  - **Camera** (the sprint's roll and nod halved on 2026-10-19, user: "screen shake seems a bit excessive"): the layer publishes `kino.fpMotion.cam = {roll, pitch, fov}` (a share of the same springs: bank into turns and strafes, the bob's roll and nod, slide and dive cant, the landing nod, the fire kick) and the movement mod's camera-feel handler applies it: roll added to its absolute `camera.rotation.z`, pitch as a difference (the mouse owns the base, `camPitch` remembers what was applied), the FOV through a new engine hook `mods.fovOffset` (engine.js adds it to the damped FOV target: +5 sprinting, +3 sliding, +2 diving). fp.js subtracts the pitch it applied last frame from its own look-rate measurement, so the kick does not read back as mouse motion.
  - **Sprint pose** for every rig (`view.sprintPose`, animation.js: no sprint clips play, the idle grip keeps running and the layer poses it; the engine's authored `sprintOffset` is skipped too). Measured off the BO7 reference (AK-27, 854x480 frame): the gun is held low and nearly level, swung left and canted, the support hand at the bottom centre, the muzzle at the centre-left; the steep look on the screen is perspective. `SPRINT = {pos:[.3,-2,1], rot:[.15,.45,.3]}`, the turn is about the grip (`FRAME.grip`, scaled by the rig) so the gun goes down rather than the muzzle up; the drop scales with the gun's length (`frame.len`, a pistol drops half) and the rig's scale; dual wield uses a symmetric drop without cant. It swings with the stride (`sw`) and lags the turn like everything else.
  - **Adaptive support hand**: the left wrist's animated position (camera space) is chased by a spring (170/19, softened by weight); the offset, saturating smoothly at .6 x weight x rig scale (a hard clamp kinked), is handed to the two-bone IK (`reachLeft`, weight .55, eased in and out over an eighth of a second) while idle, sprinting or firing, so the hand trails the gun's quick moves and settles back onto the grip. Off while reloading, meleeing, inspecting, mantling or aiming, and on rigs whose left hand is off the gun (`frame.oneHanded`: the wrist more than 18 x rig from the grip, the BO3 G18's arm hangs free).
  - **Reload**: `s.reload` eases in while `view.mode === 'reload'`: the gun comes in, down and turns toward the player.
  - **Framing**: idle guns sit further back and a touch higher (`FRAME.bo7 = (.3, .1, -2.8)`) so most of the gun shows. Imported rigs (BO3/BO7 viewmodels, told by `def.handsModel` / the model path, not by size: a BO1 China Lake or Death Machine with the hands far apart measured as imported and lost the sprint pose) keep their hand-set `def.viewOffset` placement and get only the shift, in their scale; the earlier grip normalisation fought those offsets and put a third of the BO3 guns off the screen (sweep: 93R, MP7, AK117, K9, UMG, Striker, bows). Their scale (`frame.rig`, about 2.2) comes from the left arm's length (`FRAME.arm` 23.7; the wrist spacing made a one-handed UZI measure tiny) and scales the sprint pose.
  - **Matrices**: the layer updates the viewmodel with `view.pivot.updateMatrixWorld(true)`, never `view.root`: three r185's `getWorldPosition` no longer refreshes parents, and a root update composes against the pivot's stale world matrix, so the IK blended from last frame's wrist (45% of each frame's pivot move leaked into the hand: a per-frame smear, the other half of the "jittery" report). Verified with a late `kino.mods.on('update')` hook: the wrist lands at exactly 55% of the way to the target every frame.
  - **The support hand on underbarrel attachments (2026-10-19, user: "do the hands also change when certain attachments are used too?" / "can you do both")**: a Grip, Grenade Launcher, Masterkey or Flamethrower moves the left hand from the handguard onto the part. Once per weapon and attachment set, at idle, the part's extent is measured from the gun's vertices under the attachment's shown tag (view space, kept in the gun bone's frame: that bone's own axes are not the view's) and the wrist's spot chosen per kind (`UNDER` in fp.js): a vertical grip is held at mid height from its left face, the palm a hand's length ahead of the wrist, with the wrist rolled 1.2 rad about the forearm so the fingers wrap the post; a launcher or shotgun under its forward section, which the reach clamp (95% of the arm) pulls back to where a T5 arm gets. The two-bone IK carries the hand there with the hand lag's offset on top (`handLag.lag`: the block's `off` turns into an absolute target once used), eased in and out like the hand lag and off in the same states. Imported BO3 guns' Grip is a stat-only unlock with no part: nothing changes.
  - **Sights (2026-10-19, user: "on some custom crosshairs [sights], the ads is a bit broken")**: the hip poses (sprint pose, reload tilt, slide, dive, wall-kick, mantle) are scaled by (1-aim)², gone before the reflex dot shows at aim .85: the scopes mod runs before this layer and measures the sight from the engine's pose, so a pose added afterwards moved the sight off the dot (aiming out of a sprint: the dot hung 144-373 px from the sight on the M16, AK74u and Spectre; after a reload 17-68 px). The camera keeps the slide/dive cant while aiming. In the scopes mod the dot now also waits until the sight has arrived under it (`align.err` < 0.007 rad, ≈ 6 px; it stays while aiming), which also stops the dot popping early on the 2.2× BO3 rigs, whose alignment lerp lags more; an optic with nothing to measure (the Grimhawk's rangefinder) shows it at aim .85 as before. The alignment measures the optic's lens when it has one and the Aimpoint's body only when it doesn't: the body's centroid sits 0.4 below its tube, so the red dot's window stood above the dot with the M14's rear sight under it (user: "when aiming with a sight equipped on the m14, it has the thing for the iron sights"). Measured on 18 gun/optic pairs: the dot shows within 10 px of the sight in every scenario.
  - **Mantle hand drift (fixed 2026-10-19, user: "when mantling, sometimes the hands get dispositions on the gun")**: the hand lag and the mantle reach both run in one frame; the mantle's `remember` overwrote the hand lag's snapshot with the hand lag's *result*, and `restoreArm` put that back as "the animation's" next frame, so a little of the lag baked into the arm every frame of the overlap: after each mantle the support hand sat further off the grip (M16: 1.5, 2.2, 4.6 units after 1-3 mantles). `remember` now keeps the first snapshot of a bone per frame (drift 0.005).
  - **One-handed rigs** are told by the left wrist's distance to the gun's bounding box (> 6 × rig scale), not to the grip bone: the Ray Gun's left hand really is off the gun (21 units), while the Wunderwaffe's grips the front far from `j_gun` and was losing its hand lag.
  - Tests (scratchpad, not in the repo): a per-frame sampler against the layer's own clock (`s.t`, since wall-clock frame spikes on the shared GPU fake jitter), an all-weapon sweep (idle, ADS, sprint screenshots, on-screen share, smoothness), and YouTube frame grabs of the reference clips through headless Chromium (the user's permission).
