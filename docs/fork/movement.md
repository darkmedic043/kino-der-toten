# Movement mod and stairs

- **Movement mod** (`mods/movement`): gravity 1400 (jumpSpeed recomputed for jumpHeight 39); groundAcceleration 1500, except the game's original 2600 on stairs (`state.stairs` is set for 0.35 s whenever `onFloor` flips without a jump); airAcceleration 320 only after a real jump (detected from the jump key) or 0.2 s airborne. It wraps `player.update(dt,input)`: stair snap-down (a ray ≤ stepHeight+4 after walking off an edge), a **one-button crouch** (C/Ctrl, tracked by its own key listeners in game time: tap toggles crouch, holding 0.35 s goes prone, a tap while sprinting at >220 slides; jump from crouch or prone stands; sprint cancels a toggled crouch; `input.crouch` is overridden). **Prone** swaps `crouchHeight` 30, `crouchEyeHeight` 17 and `crouchSpeed` 45 (stands only if `_canUseHeight`). **Slide** lasts 1.35 s with speed = 130 + (start−130)·(1−u)^1.7, start = min(490, h·1.6), applied after the controller step along a slowly steering dir; it ends early on a wall hit, emits a `slide` event, and plays the data.json `slideSound` file or a synthesized band-passed noise scrape, **mantle** (jump near a wall whose top is 19–74 above the feet with room to stand, checked by a ray pair plus `capsuleIntersect`: a scripted 0.28 s + rise/260 climb). Camera: head bob, landing dip only on real falls (>0.22 s airborne and vy<−260), strafe/slide/mantle roll, eased eye-height changes. The state is exposed as `kino.movement.state`. `applyStance()` in characters.js pitches the character root (prone +1.45 face-down, slide −0.85, mantle +0.35; YXZ order) and `setStance` turns the aim pose past the head when prone; co-op poses carry `k` (stance). The motionModel landing threshold is −300 (no crouch per stair).
- **Stairs** (upstream `player-controller.js` edit): a riser collision projected the walking speed away and `_tryStep` never restored it. Steps are now also tried when the last contact is a non-floor surface facing against the movement (dot < −0.3; an edge brushed while descending faces along it and is ignored), and a successful step restores the pre-collision horizontal velocity. Measured with a navmesh stair walker: Kino's real staircases climb 10–55% faster with 0–14 slow frames instead of up to 177; descents are unchanged.
- **Stair spring (2026-10-05, maps with `stairSmoothing`)**: the absorbed offset is eased by a critically damped spring (ω = 2 × `stairSmoothRate`) instead of the first-order ease, which restarted at every riser so the eye's climb speed pulsed about ±50% per step. Cheese Cube climb, real-time 60 fps: max camera second difference 2.13 → 0.55, RMS 0.195 → 0.109, eye speed steady at 1.3–1.55/frame. The eye trails the feet by about one step (12 units) while climbing and settles at the top.
- **Stair camera smoothing** (movement mod): grounded feet jumps of 1.5 to stepHeight+6 in one frame (step-ups and snap-downs) go into `stairOffset`, which the camera absorbs and eases out (rate 11/s, clamp ±24); bigger jumps reset it. Camera jerk on the big lobby-side staircase went from max 4.3 (21 frames > 1.5) to 1.5 (1 frame).
- **Slides on slopes** (movement mod): a slide starts if grounded or airborne < 0.25 s (slopes flicker), when sprinting above 170 (was 220; uphill is slower), and the start speed is at least 220 × 1.6. While sliding, the player is snapped to the ground below (reach stepHeight + speed·dt·1.5); the ground normal's horizontal part gives a downhill boost (gravity × 0.9, clamped −start to +420) and slows the slide timer downhill; the slide ends if stalled below 0.6 × endSpeed.
- **Debris smoothing** (2026-09-30): on flat ground (a down ray from the feet hits a surface with |n.y| > .985), grounded feet changes of 0.12–1.5 also go into `stairOffset`, so riding over planks and rubble doesn't jolt the camera. On ramps they pass straight through, so the camera doesn't lag the slope. Walking up to the terminal booth: max camera second difference 2.6 → 1.1.
- **Black Ops 7 movement** (2026-09-30; features per Dexerto/Destructoid: omnimovement, wall jumps, dive; tactical sprint is a perk in BO7 so it was left out):
  - Omnimovement: `game.js`/`zombies.js` sprint on Shift + any movement (not only W), and never while sliding or diving (so hip fire works mid-slide and BO1's lowered sprint pose doesn't play). Slides steer toward the input direction (`moveDir()`), not the camera.
  - Dive: holding crouch for 0.2 s while sprinting leaps along the move direction (speed max(380, 1.45 × current), 250 up), keeps its momentum in the air (air control would brake it) and lands prone. A tap still slides.
  - Wall jump (**off since 2026-10-05**, `WALL_JUMP=false`: the user found it firing when they meant to mantle): a fresh jump while airborne (>0.12 s) with a wall within radius + 20 (probed in 12 directions at feet +22 and +46) kicks off it: along-wall speed ×0.85, 290 out, 0.95 × jumpSpeed up. Max 3 per airtime, 0.28 s apart, not the same wall twice in a row; reset on landing. Events `dive`, `diveLand`, `walljump`.
  - Measured with key input on fresh pages: sideways sprint 285, slide 359 → 136, dive 362 airborne then prone by 0.5 s, wall kick 324 out and up.
- **First-person motion** (`mods/movement/fp.js`, added to the pivot after `view.update`): look inertia (springs on yaw/pitch deltas), strafe roll, takeoff sink and landing dip, slide cant, dive tuck, wall-jump kick; aiming damps most of it. Mantle: the gun pitches away around the camera (not translated, so the left shoulder stays within reach), and the left arm reaches for the ledge with two-bone IK. The target is `mt.hand` (ledge edge, 7 left) in camera space, clamped into view (z −12…−16, 7–19° down, 5–24° left), because the real ledge is usually below the frame and beyond the arm's ~22 reach. The wrist and fingers ease to the skeleton's bind pose (an open hand). The rig's animations don't key every arm bone, so IK edits are restored each frame (`restoreArm`/`sealArm`); otherwise they pile up. Third person and co-op have a `dive` stance (flat, pitch 1.25).
- **Sprint stair snags (2026-10-05, upstream `player-controller.js`)**: sprinting, the rounded capsule rides up a riser's edge and leaves the floor with a small upward speed; the step-up required `climbSpeedY<=0` and floor/coyote, so the next riser stopped the player (logged: every snag was a skipped step with vy 9–109 and coyote 0). Steps are now also tried when airborne under 0.35 s with vy ≤ 160 and no jump in the last 0.6 s (`_airTime`, `_jumpGuard`). Cheese Cube, 10 stair runs sprinting at 30 fps: speed dips below 45% went from 13 to 3, climbs ~15% faster; walking at 60 fps no dips (1 before).
- **Camera feel guard (2026-10-17)**: the bob/roll/dip/stair state in the movement mod's update handler read the camera back every frame, so one non-finite value (a `camera.lookAt` with a bad argument in a test harness did it) stayed forever and took the player's velocity with it. The handler now reports the state once (`[movement] non-finite camera feel` in the console, with every input) and resets it.
